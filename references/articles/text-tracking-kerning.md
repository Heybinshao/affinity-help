3.   Tracking and kerning

Affinity allows you to precisely adjust the spacing between characters using tracking and kerning.

The letter spacing in a paragraph of any alignment can have a subtle impact on the reader. Affinity allows you to experiment with the tracking of your text.

![Image 1: After](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3OLOgSN5YIIIhrVmICnPRW/fcbddfc18e893af521d187a4b9100338/tracking_after.jpg)

![Image 2: Before](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4QRbtwMoA5w9rgyyXBd0Oz/1c04c1e9ea48bb4cb63b3fb3e72afac9/tracking_before.jpg)

By default, Affinity uses the character spacing built into the font by its designer (metric kerning). These values are stored in the font as a lookup table (kerning table), and are optimised for most situations—but manual kerning lets you override them where needed.

Manual kerning may be required where two large, adjacent characters look too far apart, or too close, due to the shape of the characters. The technique is often used in large text to ensure that all characters appear evenly spaced. Examples include titles, headlines, and text logos.

1.   If the **Character** panel isn't already open, on the **Window** menu, select **Text > Character**.
2.   Select a range of text.
3.   On the **Character** panel's **Position & Transform** section, adjust **Tracking**![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6KwrcFKswnVZXVgMtahwYA/b04b5849b7aeef9f5bbf119caf40e621/char_tracking.svg)  as required.

Positive values loosen (increase) spacing between each pair of characters in the selection. Negative values tighten (decrease) spacing.

1.   If the **Character** panel isn't already open, on the **Window** menu, select **Text > Character**.
2.   Make an insertion point between two characters.
3.   On the **Character** panel's **Position & Transform** section, set **Kerning**![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/qJlAdXboHkaI0VClcBgZO/34340da6a39ff45635093c09a448db0b/char_kerning.svg)  as required.

Positive values loosen (increase) spacing between the characters either side of the insertion point. Negative values tighten (decrease) spacing.

The following keyboard shortcuts also allow you to adjust tracking or kerning:

*    To loosen spacing in 10 per mille (‰) increments, press the **⌥****→** keys (Mac) / **Alt**+**Right** keys (Windows). 
*    To tighten spacing in 10‰ decrements, press the **⌥****←** keys (Mac) / **Alt**+**Left** keys (Windows). 
*    To loosen spacing in 50‰ increments, press the **⌥****⌘****→** keys (Mac) / **Alt**+**Shift**+**Right** keys (Windows). 
*    To tighten spacing in 50‰ decrements, press the **⌥****⌘****←** keys (Mac) / **Alt**+**Shift**+**Left** keys (Windows).
