# Create and log in to your Canva account

You can set up a Canva account in different ways. Continue with your email address, Google, Facebook, or other methods to sign up and start using Canva for free. If you already have an account, we’ll log you in.

Have an account but can’t sign in? [Get help with common login issues](https://www.canva.com/help/cant-log-in).

Are you a teacher, school, or nonprofit organization?

There are special versions of Canva just for you. Learn about [Canva Education](https://www.affinity.studio/help/about-canva-for-education/) or [Canva for Nonprofits](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-for-nonprofits/).

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/sign-up-log-in/#create-an-account-with-your-email-address)

### Create an account with your email address

1.   Go to [canva.com](https://www.canva.com/) and select **Sign up**.
2.   On the signup page, choose to **Continue with email** or select **Continue another way**, then **Continue with work email**.
3.   Enter the email address you’d like to use in Canva, then select **Continue**.Please note that we don't allow temporary or disposable email addresses. 
4.   Enter your preferred account name.
5.   Select **Continue.**
6.   Enter the code that was sent to your email address to finish signing up.
7.   You can set a password in Settings later. Learn how to set or [change your password.](https://www.affinity.studio/help/change-password/)
8.   Verify your email address by following the steps we'll send to your email.
9.   Enter the confirmation code from the email you received.You can also sign up using Google, Facebook, Apple, or other methods. 

You’re all set! Log in anytime with the email you set.

Forgot your password? You can [reset it anytime](https://www.affinity.studio/help/change-password/). You can also learn tips on [protecting your account](https://www.canva.com/help/protect-account).

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/sign-up-log-in/#create-an-account-with-your-facebook--google--apple-or-other-methods)

### Create an account with your Facebook, Google, Apple or other methods

You can use one of your other accounts (Facebook, Google, Apple, and others) to create a Canva account.

From the signup or login page, or from your Canva app, choose**Log in/Continue with...** . You may have to log in or enter authentication codes related to your chosen account if prompted.

Here are the methods we currently support:

*   Apple
*   Facebook
*   Google
*   Email
*   Microsoft
*   LINE (Only available in Japan)
*   Yahoo! (Only available in Japan)
*   Work email address

**Note:** Some methods may not be available for some countries.

### Log in to your Canva account

You can log in using the same methods above:

*   Log in with your email address or work email address
*   Log in using Facebook, Google, Apple or other methods
*   In some countries, you can also log in with your mobile number

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/sign-up-log-in/#access-affinity)

### Access Affinity

You need a Canva account to access Affinity. Learn [how to access Affinity using Canva](https://www.affinity.studio/help/free-affinity-access/).

If you’re having issues logging in to Affinity using your Canva account, please [contact us](https://support.affinity.studio/hc/requests/new).
