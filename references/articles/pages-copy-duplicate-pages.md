# Copy and duplicate pages

You can quickly create copies of pages on the Pages panel.

There are two ways to do this:

*   **Duplicate** pages immediately after the last page in your selection.
*   **Copy** the selected pages to your chosen position, before or after a specific page number.

The selected pages need not be a continuous range.

The order of the new pages will match the originals.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/pages-copy-duplicate-pages/#copying-pages-using-drag-and-drop)

## Copying pages using drag and drop

Pages can be copied by holding the **⌥****key** (Mac) / **Ctrl****key** (Windows) while dragging and dropping their thumbnails.

When you drag page thumbnails:

*   A blue line shows where the pages will go when dropped.
*   The cursor indicates whether dropping will start a new spread or extend an existing one.
*   Where pages can go depends on whether **Reflow Pages** (on the Pages panel's preferences menu) is enabled or disabled.

When Reflow Pages is enabled, pages reflow starting from whichever comes first—either the first page in the selection or the drop location—through to the end of your document.

When Reflow Pages is disabled, pages will not automatically reflow. This can create “ghost pages”.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/pages-copy-duplicate-pages/#how-to-duplicate-pages)

### How to copy pages (using drag and drop)

Desktop app

On the **Pages** panel:

1.   **⌥**-drag (Mac) / **Ctrl**-drag (Windows) one or more publication pages or a master page.
2.   Hover where you want to copy the pages and drop when the cursor shows the required action will happen.

When copying master pages, be careful not to drop one master onto another, which will apply the first to the second.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/pages-copy-duplicate-pages/#how-to-copy-publication-pages--via-dialog-)

### How to copy publication pages (via dialog)

Desktop app

1.   On the **Pages** panel:
    1.   Select one or more publication pages 1.
    2.   **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) on the selection and choose **Move/Copy Pages**.

2.   On the dialog that appears:
    1.   Specify the destination **Page** number, and whether to position the new pages **Before** or **After** it.
    2.   Select **Copy**.
    3.   Select **OK**.

1 Alternatively, type the page numbers later on the dialog.
