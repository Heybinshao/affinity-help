# Capitalization - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/text-capitalisation/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Design fundamentals](https://www.affinity.studio/help/design-fundamentals/)
3.   Capitalization

Affinity can alter the capitalization of selected words to suit one of several use cases.

The following capitalizations are available on the **Text** menu, under **Capitalization**

When you use dynamic capitalizations, Affinity keeps the original letter case that was typed and changes how the letter appears on the fly. This allows you to revert to the original capitalization if needed.

*   **None**—removes whichever of the following two capitalizations is already applied to the selection.
*   **Small Caps**—each letter is displayed using small-caps glyphs, if the applied OpenType font includes them; if unavailable, upper-case glyphs are scaled down to the appropriate height.
*   **All Caps**—all letters are displayed as upper-case glyphs.

![Image 1: Dynamic capitalizations](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/FYdR1YmM9QwxLbRqaAzw9/b940ae190138aeef1114933a064e7fc9/capitalisation_01.jpg)

An example sentence as typed (A) and with (B) Small Caps and (C) All Caps dynamic capitalizations applied.

Additional dynamic capitalizations—e.g. Case Sensitive Forms—may be available on the **Typography** panel. These are only available if your chosen OpenType font supports them.

When you apply a persistent capitalization, Affinity changes the letter cases in your text selection and saved in your document, not just how they look.

*   **Lower Case**—all letters in the selection become lower-case glyphs.
*   **Upper Case**—all letters in the selection become upper-case glyphs.
*   **Toggle Case**—upper-case letters in the selection become their lower-case equivalent and vice versa.
*   **Title Case**—each word starts with an upper-case letter, unless the word is a title exception or any of its letters are upper case already. See below for precise details.
*   **Sentence Case**—each word begins with a lower-case letter, except the first word and any proper nouns.

![Image 2: Persistent capitalizations](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7HZkGB7D6Jnf0RfdLXLzMi/d8aa879b6876d02c6b10589f7512ebcb/capitalisation_02.jpg)

An example sentence with (D) Lower Case, (E) Upper Case, (F) Toggle Case, (G) Title Case and (H) Sentence Case capitalizations applied.

When the **Title Case** or **Sentence Case** capitalization is applied, the text selection changes to only words in which capitalization was changed.

When the Title Case capitalization is applied, words in your text selection are affected as follows (in order):

*   A word that contains any upper-case letters is considered to be a proper noun, scientific or technical term, or an acronym. None of its letters' cases are changed 1.
*   A word that is not a title exception for the specified language has its first letter converted to upper case.
*   A word that is a title exception and first in a sentence or paragraph 2 has its first letter converted to upper case.
*   A word that is a title exception and not first in a sentence or paragraph is made entirely lower case.

1 Words with upper-case letters past their first are unaffected on the first application of the Title Case capitalization. Applying the capitalization a second time makes their first letter upper case and all others lower case.

2 When your text selection contains multiple sentences, rules concerning first words apply to each word that appears after a line break, paragraph break, or appropriate punctuation.

The **Title Exceptions** section of Affinity's Settings contains a list of short prepositions, conjunctions, articles, and other words that aren't altered by the Title Case capitalization—except if they are the first word in your text selection.

You can use the language pop-up menu to set exceptions for different languages. The Title Case capitalization uses the exceptions that correspond to the selected text's **Spelling** setting on the **Character** panel's **Language** section.

With text selected:

*   On the **Text** menu, hover over **Capitalization**, then select the required capitalization effect.

The **Small Caps** and **All Caps** capitalizations can be removed, reverting text to its original typed case, by selecting **None** on the same menu. Other options change the case of letters saved in your document.

In Affinity's **Settings**:

1.   Select **Title Exceptions**.
2.   On the pop-up menu, select the language for the exception.
3.   In the box below, type the word you want to remain unchanged by the Title Case capitalization (except when it is the first word).
4.   Click **Add**.

In Affinity's **Settings**:

1.   Select **Title Exceptions**.
2.   On the pop-up menu, select the language of the exception.
3.   In the list of exceptions, select the unwanted entry.
4.   Click **Remove**.
