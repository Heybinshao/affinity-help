# Editing tables - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/tables-edit-tables/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Page layout](https://www.affinity.studio/help/page-layout/)
3.   Editing tables

The formatting of tables can be easily adjusted to suit your needs.

*   On the **Window** menu, select **Table > Table**.

1.   Select the **Table Tool**![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/U9027CHEs8YJPz3HslFFN/21c88099e755bed558908ec8f6cbd427/table_text_tool.svg) .
2.   Click an existing table on the page.

With a table selected, do one of the following:

*   For an individual row or column, click its header.
*   For multiple adjacent rows or columns, drag from the headers of the first and last that are required.
*   For multiple non-adjacent rows or columns, **⌘**-click (Mac) / **Ctrl**-click (Windows) the header of each required row or column.

*   For an individual cell: 
    1.   Do one of the following: 
        *   Drag from the required cell into an adjacent cell, then drag back to the required cell. The cell's contents and borders are selected.
        *   Make an insertion point within the required cell. Nothing is yet selected.

    2.   On the **Table** panel's **Stroke and Fill** section, click **Inside**![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3MvMxPvnbvXU5fPYqb625h/bc6c8b3cea47263d66d94e04d4f07a45/inside.svg)  to leave only the cell's contents selected.

*   For multiple adjacent cells: 
    *   Drag from the first required cell to the last required cell. The cells can be in different rows/columns.

*   For multiple non-adjacent cells: 
    1.   Create an insertion point in the first required cell.
    2.   Hold the **⌘** key (Mac) / **Ctrl** key (Windows) and click each additional required cells.

Selecting a cell also selects its contents.

Selected cells are highlighted in light blue. Selected cell borders are indicated by a dark blue stroke.

With the Table Tool

![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/U9027CHEs8YJPz3HslFFN/21c88099e755bed558908ec8f6cbd427/table_text_tool.svg)

 selected, do any of the following:

*   Double-click a word.
*   Triple-click a line.
*   Quadruple-click a paragraph.
*   Quintuple-click for all text.
*   Drag over the text you want.

Do one of the following:

*   To add/delete at a specific position, hover the pointer over a row or column header, click the arrow that appears and select: 
    *   **Insert Row** / **Insert Column**—to insert a new row or column above/to the left, respectively.
    *   **Delete Row** / **Delete Column**—to delete the row or column.

*   To add/delete at the end of the table and maintain overall table dimensions, click the arrows at the end of the row headers ![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7KnXL9epQSXyDJWN5AAaSg/33548db590dc9e17d4b4b231005ce4f4/table_row_arrows.png)  or column headers ![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/019AW7LevoROIYOoAeqBix/c9735d426449621b2129f72a8b40685c/table_column_arrows.png)  and edit the number in the box that appears.
*   To add/delete at the end of the table and affect overall table dimensions, drag the arrows at the end of the row headers or column headers.

*    Drag the table outwards from one of the handles in the bottom-right corner to expand it, or inwards to reduce its size: 
    *   To rescale both the table and its contents, drag the outer handle.
    *   To rescale the table and flow text, drag the inner handle.

1.   Make an insertion point in the table.
2.    Do one of the following: 
    *   To resize without affecting overall table dimensions, drag from the separator below/right of the header of the row/column you want to resize.
    *    To resize while adding to/removing from overall table dimensions, hold the **⇧** key (Mac) / **Shift** key (Windows) and drag from the separator below/right of the header of the row/column you want to resize. 

Do one of the following:

1.   Click a row/column header and select **Autofit Row to Contents** / **Autofit Column to Contents**.
2.   Double-click on a table's bottom or right edge handle to automatically adjust the table's height or width to fit its contents.

1.   Select adjacent columns or rows you wish to affect.
2.   **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) within the selected cells and choose **Evenly Distribute Columns** or **Evenly Distribute Rows**. 

1.   With the **Table Tool**![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/U9027CHEs8YJPz3HslFFN/21c88099e755bed558908ec8f6cbd427/table_text_tool.svg)  selected, click within a cell to make an insertion point.
2.   Type the required text.

The cell expands vertically as needed to accommodate multiple lines of text.

With the required rows, columns, or cells selected, do one of the following:

*   On the **Table** panel's **Stroke and Fill** section: 
    1.   Click the **Cell Fill** or **Cell Stroke Fill** option.
    2.   Select a color.

*   On the **Color** or **Swatches** panel: 
    1.   Select the **Fill** or **Stroke** color selector.
    2.   Select a color.

1.   Select the cells whose borders you wish to affect.
2.   On the **Table** panel's **Stroke and Fill** section: 
    1.   Select the required **Border** option.
    2.   Set the **Cell Stroke Fill** and **Cell Stroke** options as required.

Selected cells are highlighted in light blue. Selected cell borders are indicated by a dark blue stroke.

1.   Make an insertion point within the cell.
2.   On the **Text** menu, select **Insert > Spaces and Tabs > Tab**.

For Mac: Alternatively, hold the **⌃(ctrl)⌥** keys and press the **⇥** key.

With table text or cells selected, do any of the following:

*   Use the context toolbar to apply a font family, traits and color, paragraph and vertical alignment, and other common text features, or to apply text styles.
*   Use the **Table** panel's **Cell** section to inset content from its cell's edges.
*   Use the **Table** panel's **Rotation** section to set the orientation of cell contents.
*   Use the **Table** panel's **Baseline Grid** section to space the table's lines of text consistently.

1.   Select table cells or a range of table text.
2.   On the context toolbar, select horizontal and vertical text alignment options as required.

1.   Select table cells or a range of table text.
2.    Press the **⌫** key (Mac) / **Backspace** key (Windows). 

*    To merge adjacent cells: 
    1.   Select the cells.
    2.   **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) the selection.
    3.   On the menu that appears, select **Merge Cells**.

*    To merge all cells in a column/row: 
    1.   Hover over the column/row header, then click the arrow that appears.
    2.   On the menu that appears, select **Merge Column** / **Merge Row**.

Do one of the following:

*    To separate a merged cell: 
    1.   Select the cell.
    2.   **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) the selection.
    3.   On the menu that appears, select **Separate Cells**.

*    To separate all merged cells in a column/row: 
    1.   Select the column/row.
    2.   Hover over the column/row header, then click the arrow that appears to the right of its label.
    3.   Select **Separate Cells**.
