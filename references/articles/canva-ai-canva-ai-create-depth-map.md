# Create Depth Map - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-create-depth-map/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Canva integrations](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-integrations/)
3.   Create Depth Map

Use **Detect Depth**

![Image 1: Create Depth Map](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3EWZYFDr7YAYWhOSse6jOO/0dab739dbb4b4ab36108039128b9073a/create_depth_map.svg)

 to evaluate an image's depth based on focal range.

![Image 2: After applying Create Depth Map.](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/29eSW2za0JQ5u2RRMUtX1U/a251a79945e617fc1b4006db60dadce9/detectDepth_after.jpg)

![Image 3: Before applying Create Depth Map.](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/Uem5bc0DA0nKVp4j4Vf6j/5d8fd9809148e792ade5a6cc1dc13533/detectDepth_before.jpg)

1.   In the **Canva AI Studio,**on the **Layers** panel, select the image layer you want to analyze.
2.   On the top-right of the context toolbar, click **Detect Depth**![Image 4: Create Depth Map](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3EWZYFDr7YAYWhOSse6jOO/0dab739dbb4b4ab36108039128b9073a/create_depth_map.svg) .
3.   Wait for Canva AI to analyse the image and generate the depth map.
4.   Review the result to identify how areas of the image have been mapped by focal distance.
5.   (Optional) Use the generated depth map as a reference for masking, selections, or further local edits.

**Detect Depth / Depth**(as a filter)is a premium feature

![Image 5: Canva Pro Feature](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3cDfFrl1AFrPewvudOOxnG/2310131f39f4a6dd67092f007be0b1b9/crown.svg)

. To use it, you need to be subscribed to the Canva Pro plan. To learn more about the premium features usage, visit the [Canva AI & Affinity ML](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-affinity-ml/) topic.

**Detect Depth**

![Image 6: Create Depth Map](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3EWZYFDr7YAYWhOSse6jOO/0dab739dbb4b4ab36108039128b9073a/create_depth_map.svg)

 uses machine learning to map and evaluate the scene based on focal range distance. The feature can be used as a precursor to masking and working with isolated image areas, in preparation for further editing. It saves time especially in compositions of linear nature, landscapes with a foreground, middle ground and background areas for instance, where a quick selection can be made to isolate the said area by its capture position and distance from the camera lens.

The same functionality is offered in Affinity by the **Depth** filter. Visit the dedicated topic to learn more.

As with other Canva AI features, **Create Depth Map**

![Image 7: Create Depth Map](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3EWZYFDr7YAYWhOSse6jOO/0dab739dbb4b4ab36108039128b9073a/create_depth_map.svg)

 is applied directly onto your image layer. We recommended applying it on a duplicate layer which may be optionally renamed. This aids non-destructive workflows where a quick point of reference to the focal range may be toggled on and off without affecting the Background layer.
