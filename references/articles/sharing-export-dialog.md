# Export dialog - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/sharing-export-dialog/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Export, share, and publish](https://www.affinity.studio/help/export-share-publish/)
3.   Export dialog

Use the Export dialog when you need to export your document in whole or in part, with full control of settings.

Your choice of file format determines which other settings are presented on the right side of the Export dialog.

The most common settings you might want to edit are in the **File** section. Below, the **Advanced** section contains any additional settings.

After changing settings, you can save your choices as a new preset for use in Quick Export, the Export dialog, and the Slice Studio.

For descriptions of the complete range of settings and their availability by file format, see [Export settings](https://www.affinity.studio/help/sharing-export-settings/).

The **Area** export option determines which parts of a document are included in exported output. This can be all pages/spreads or artboards, only those you explicitly specify, or based on the selected objects.

The following options are available:

*   **Whole document**—the output will include the current page (for one-page documents) or all artboards.
*   **All Pages** / **All Spreads**—the output will include all pages or spreads, or only those specified using the Pages option.
*   **Current Page** / **Current Spread**—the output will include only the current page or spread.
*   **Selection Area**—the output includes other layers that overlap the selection.
*   **Selection Only**—the output excludes other layers that overlap the selection.
*   Named artboard—the output includes only the artboard selected from the menu.

When the option is set to _All spreads_ or _All pages_, the **Pages** option becomes available. Type the page numbers to include in the exported output. For example, explicit page numbers like _4,5,6_, a range like _4-17_, or an arbitrary mix like _4-5, 19, 27, 39_.

If your exported file contains fewer pages than expected, check the _Area_ export setting is set to _Whole document_, _All Pages_, or _All Spreads_.

1.   On the **File** menu, select **Export > Export**.
2.   On the dialog that appears: 
    1.   On the left, select a preset.
    2.   (Optional) Inspect the preview and adjust settings as needed.
    3.   At the bottom right, select _Ask for Location_ on the pop-up menu.
    4.   (Optional) To open the file's location after export, enable **Show in Finder** (Mac) / **Show in Explorer** (Windows).
    5.   Click **Export**.
    6.   Name the file, choose where to save it, then click **Save**.

1.   On the **File** menu, select **Export > Export**.
2.   On the dialog that appears: 
    1.   On the left, select a preset as the starting point for your custom preset.
    2.   On the right: 
        1.   The selected preset's name appears at the top. Adjust the settings below as needed.
        2.   Click **Preferences**![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6UxpXBt5miovw347YaOPUz/6f8e3e8d9b379daa0bdc5a3ac6ff23e8/panel_preferences.svg) , then select **Create Preset**.

    3.   Type a name for your preset, then click **OK**.

From now on, the preset is available on the left of the Export dialog, in Quick Export, and in the Slice Studio.

1.   On the **File** menu, select **Export > Export**.
2.   On the dialog that appears: 
    1.   Find the preset in the list on the left, then **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) on it.
    2.   Do one of the following: 
        *   Select **Rename**, type a new name, then click **OK**.
        *   Select **Delete**, then click **Yes** to confirm.

A preset can also be renamed or deleted by clicking the arrow next to it at the top right of the dialog, then selecting **Rename Preset** or **Delete Preset**.

When previewing your export, the following modifier keys can be used to zoom in to areas of interest and view the results of different settings applied in the dialog:

*   To zoom in or out of the preview, hold the **⌥** key (Mac) / **Ctrl** key (Windows) while using a mouse scroll wheel or trackpad.
*   To pan the zoomed-in preview using a traditional mouse scroll wheel or trackpad: 
    *   up or down, scroll as normal.
    *   left or right, hold the **⇧** key (Mac) / **Shift** key (Windows) and scroll.

*   To zoom in by 10% increments, press **⌘+** (Mac) / **Ctrl+** (Windows).
*   To zoom out by 10% decrements, press **⌘-** (Mac) / **Ctrl-** (Windows).
*   To zoom to a specific magnification percentage, hold the **⌘** key (Mac) / **Ctrl** key (Windows) and press: 
    *   the **1** key to zoom to 100%.
    *   the **2** key to zoom to 200%.
    *   the **3** key to zoom to 400%.
    *   the **4** key to zoom to 800%.

*   To zoom to fit, press **⌘0** (Mac) / **Ctrl**+**0** (Windows). On Windows, you can also double-click the image preview.
