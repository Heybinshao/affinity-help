## Freezing areas to make a mask

There may be areas of your image which you want to protect from being warped by the Liquify tools. You can do this by 'freezing' those areas using the Liquify Freeze Tool. Frozen areas can be thawed using the Liquify Thaw Tool, if you wish for them to be subject to warps once more.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/liquify-persona-liquify-masking/#how-to-apply-a-mask)

### How to apply a mask

Desktop app

Do one of the following:

*   With the **Liquify Freeze Tool**  ![Image 10](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2O0mOSqCH97d8hBihIRRw4/69c04a2bb8d41212e079c7757caf1d93/liquify_freeze_tool.svg)        selected, drag on the page to select areas to be masked.
*   On the **Mask** panel, select **Mask All** to apply a mask to the entire image.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/liquify-persona-liquify-masking/#how-to-remove-areas-of-a-mask)

### How to remove areas of a mask

Desktop app

Do one of the following:

*   With the **Liquify Thaw Tool**  ![Image 11](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/36CGSzbCevhwbVYIXk0WFM/9915b49972d2d039bfc6e7a524647dea/liquify_thaw_tool.svg)        selected, drag on the page to remove areas from a mask.
*   On the **Mask** panel, select **Clear Mask** to remove a mask entirely from the image.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/liquify-persona-liquify-masking/#how-to-invert-masked-areas)

### How to invert masked areas

Desktop app

*   On the **Mask** panel, select **Invert Mask**  ![Image 12](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7EvrHs0D7aRZyfd5rTJYgB/dfb8d9070a85cb73a814c4c0c173c755/channel_invert.svg)       .

To mask (or freeze) large areas of your image:

*   On the **Mask** panel, select **Mask All** and then use the **Liquify Thaw Tool** to remove parts of the mask.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/liquify-persona-liquify-masking/#see-also:)
