# Importing Affinity content into DaVinci Resolve - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/extras-davinci-affinity/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Working with other apps and devices](https://www.affinity.studio/help/working-with-other-apps-and-devices/)
3.   Importing Affinity content into DaVinci Resolve

In the world of video editing, you can utilise Affinity content directly within DaVinci Resolve. Projects are enhanced by adding an Affinity document to the video project's timeline tracks.

The import lets motion designers and video editors use Affinity for pro-level design assets in conjunction with DaVinci Resolve for video production. Great for introducing a highly designed end screen (end card) to your video projects bound for social media channels, e.g. YouTube.

An Affinity-designed end card placed on a YouTube-bound project's timeline.

Some other suggested uses of Affinity-sourced assets include:

*   Intros/outros
*   Overlays
*   Layer masks (painted or gradient)
*   Titles and captions (vector warped)
*   Annotation buttons
*   Logos
*   Callouts

Key benefits include:

*   Simplified video development workflows that require design assets
*   Extend pro-design capabilities by using Affinity
*   Timeline's tracks refresh in realtime
*   Assets automatically update on the timeline when edited in the external Affinity document.
*   Avoids laborious import/export round trips
*   No file conversions
*   Split individual layers onto separate tracks
*   Apply keyframes to assets for motion

Affinity's user interface is unaffected by this DaVinci Resolve interworking solution.

Blackmagic Design, the publisher of DaVinci Resolve, has its own pricing plans, separate to those of Canva. Affinity by Canva is a free app, forever. To use, you must be using the DaVinci Resolve 21 (or later) and Affinity March ’26 (or later).

1.   Import an Affinity document into DaVinci Resolve's Media Pool.
2.   Add it to the timeline.

*   Once the Affinity document is added to the timeline, right-click it and choose **Split Layers**.

The split layers can then be animated independently of other layers, even when the layer's content is edited externally in Affinity. The content updates in realtime.
