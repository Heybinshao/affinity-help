# Upgrading to Canva Pro or Canva Teams - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/upgrade-to-canva-pro-or-business/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Account and billing](https://www.affinity.studio/help/account-billing/)
3.   Upgrading to Canva Pro or Canva Teams

**Canva Pro** is the perfect plan for solo creators who want premium editing tools and templates to create faster and design like a pro.

**Canva Business**is built for individuals and growing teams who want to power their business growth. It includes everything in Canva Pro, plus higher [AI access,](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-access) stronger brand management tools, and smarter marketing with ad analytics - helping you scale with confidence. Learn more about [Canva Business.](http://canva.com/canva-business)

Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups or upgrades. The information related to Teams on this page only applies to existing Canva Teams subscribers. Learn more about the latest changes to [Canva Teams](https://www.affinity.studio/help/about-canva-teams/).

To compare available plans and see what's included, visit our[pricing page](https://www.canva.com/pricing/).

New subscribers can upgrade to Canva Business via web browser and mobile browser.

You can't upgrade in the mobile app yet. This feature is coming soon.

If you subscribed to Canva Pro through the mobile app and want to add a larger team, [cancel your current Apple or Google subscription](https://www.affinity.studio/help/cancel-canva-plan/) first, then upgrade via web or mobile browser.

1.   From the homepage, select **Upgrade your plan** or **Start your free trial**if you’re eligible.
2.   Select **Canva Pro**or**Canva Business,**then select **Upgrade.**If you’re eligible for trial, select **Try it free for 30 days.**
3.   Select a payment plan. Amounts displayed will vary per region. Learn more about our [payment plans](https://www.canva.com/help/subscription-options).
4.   Select **Next**.
5.   Select a payment option. Learn more about [Supported payment options.](https://www.affinity.studio/help/payment-options/)
6.   Enter your payment details and complete the upgrade.

**What to expect**

*   Canva Pro or Canva Business will **automatically renew** after your paid period or when your free trial ends.
*   Any work created in Canva **can’t be trademarked** or limited to your brand/team.
*   If available, your Canva Pro or Canva Business free trial will be valid for the trial period shown on the upgrade prompt.
*   **For Canva Business owners and administrators:**You’ll be charged for every person you invite or join your team (See our [pricing page](https://www.canva.com/pricing/) for more details). You’ll be able to review the charges related to adding new people before getting charged for your next billing cycle. Learn more about [Billing for Canva Business](https://www.canva.com/help/canva-teams-billing).
*   **Creating a team** will require you to subscribe to Canva Business. Learn more about [Creating teams](https://www.canva.com/help/create-teams).
*   Canva premium plan holders can unlock incredibly powerful Canva AI tools such as Generative Fill, Generative Expand, Generate Image/Vector, and more — directly in Affinity. Learn more about [accessing Affinity for free with a Canva account](https://www.affinity.studio/help/free-affinity-access/)
*   If you subscribed to Canva Pro or Canva Teams via Apple or Google, you can still access it on your computer or other devices, as long as you're signed into the same account.

When Canva Pro or Canva Business renews after your trial period, or after your paid month or year, you will be charged. The charge may appear as any of the following:

*   Canva
*   Canva*invoice_id
*   APPLE*Canva
*   GOOGLE*Canva

To avoid unplanned charges, [cancel your plan](https://www.affinity.studio/help/cancel-canva-plan/) any time **before it renews**. Canva charges follow the GMT timezone.
