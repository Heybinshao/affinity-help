3.   About Canva Enterprise

Canva Enterprise empowers large teams and organizations to design, collaborate, and manage brand assets at scale, all while maintaining security, compliance, and brand consistency.

Canva Enterprise is built for organizations with advanced needs around security, team management, and brand control. It's ideal for:

*   Organizations working across multiple departments, brands, or regions
*   Admins and brand managers who oversee company-wide design systems
*   Teams balancing creative flexibility with brand identity

Canva Enterprise supports content creation at scale while helping organizations maintain brand consistency, security, and compliance. Here are some of the main features:

| Feature | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Multi-team structure** | Set up Canva to mirror your organization and manage teams effortlessly. |
| **Brand governance at scale** | Lock logos, fonts, and colors, assign design approvers, and maintain consistent branding across every asset. |
| **Enterprise-grade security** | Use these advanced security tools to help maintain compliance, streamline user management, and support organizational security policies. - **Single Sign-On (SSO)**, **- SCIM user provisioning**, **- Audit logs**, and **IP indemnity** |
| **Seamless integrations** | Connect Canva to your existing tools and workflows using **APIs**, **SDKs**, and **custom integrations**. |
| **Dedicated onboarding and Support** | Get support with **onboarding, template setup, and training** to help your teams use Canva Enterprise effectively from day one. |
| **Access to Affinity** | Use of professional creative photo editing, layout, and illustration. Learn [how to access Affinity](https://www.affinity.studio/help/free-affinity-access/). |

Canva Business is a plan built to help individuals and growing teams power their business growth, while Canva Enterprise offers full organizational control, brand governance, and enterprise-level security. To see a detailed comparison, check out our [pricing page](http://canva.com/pricing).

At the highest level, Canva Enterprise uses an **Organization** structure that houses all users, teams, and settings.

Within the Organization, you can create multiple **Teams**, structured by department, project, region, or other groupings that align with how your organization operates.

*   **Organization admins** oversee the entire organization, including billing, brand controls, user provisioning, and team creation.
*   **Team admins** manage their assigned teams, with responsibilities limited to team members, projects, and content; they don't have access to organization-wide settings. 

Check out, [Manage teams and organization structure in Canva Enterprise](https://www.canva.com/help/enterprise-manage-teams-organization-structure) to learn more.
