# Threshold adjustment

The Threshold adjustment

![Image 8](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5TBB2jnG1R2cTXv3FpYWIU/981f5fed72b7c7c91b567422f9fd536a/threshold_adjustment_type.svg)

 allows you to create two-tone, black and white scenes from grayscale or color compositions based on pixel intensity.

After

Before

![Image 9: After](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7ICgG5egOW4UcijbMFQJs8/2e407662548264597d6c2da2221277f6/adjustment_threshold_after.jpg)

![Image 10: Black & White before](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5NXgmzFzXKve50c596UktV/3587c26a6e00f462f4d50c450a098ea1/adjustment_base.jpg)

Any pixels lighter than the threshold are converted to white; any pixels darker than the threshold are converted to black.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/adjustments-adjustment-threshold/#settings)

### Settings

Desktop app

*   **Threshold**—controls the transition point between pixels being converted to black or white. Drag the slider to the left to convert more pixels to white, drag the slider to the right to convert more pixels to black.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/adjustments-adjustment-threshold/#how-to-apply-the-adjustment)

### How to apply the adjustment

Desktop app

*   On the **Layers** panel, click **Adjustments**  ![Image 11](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4ZFx9I9M7MiUBPmytBqZrt/cad5a29a5e39b1b3faa42c5fa29402f3/add_adjustment_layer.svg)        and select **Threshold**.
*   On the **Adjustment** panel, select **Threshold**  ![Image 12](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5TBB2jnG1R2cTXv3FpYWIU/981f5fed72b7c7c91b567422f9fd536a/threshold_adjustment_type.svg)        and then click on a preset to modify, as required.
*   On the **Pixel** menu, select **New Adjustment Layer > Threshold**.

The **Adjustment** panel is not visible by default. To enable it, navigate to the top menu and select **Window > Pixel > Adjustment**.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/adjustments-adjustment-threshold/#see-also:)
