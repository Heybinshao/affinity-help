## About Focus Peaking

Focus Peaking is a visual aid that identifies regions of high detail by applying a coloured overlay to areas that are in focus. This allows you to confirm whether critical subjects—such as eyes in portraits or key elements in a scene—are sharp before continuing with further edits.

Focus Peaking analyses contrast within the image and highlights areas where edge detail is strongest. These areas are overlaid with a selectable colour, allowing you to distinguish in-focus regions from softer areas.

Use Focus Peaking when reviewing RAW images in Develop Studio, checking focus before applying sharpening or detail adjustments, evaluating images captured with shallow depth of field, or comparing multiple shots to select the sharpest image.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/focus-peaking/#settings)

### Settings

Desktop app

The following settings and options are available:

![Image 7: Focus Peaking](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5MO22yxxotQ0LUieMia3K8/001c9db747c22fa865dd48af3611c1e2/focus_peaking.svg)

**Focus Peaking**—enables or disables the overlay used to identify areas of sharp focus.
**Overlay color**—choose from red, green, blue, yellow, or purple. Select a colour that contrasts clearly with the image to help evaluate focus more accurately.

Zoom into critical areas of the image to verify focus accuracy more precisely.

Focus Peaking is a visual aid only and does not alter the image.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/focus-peaking/#how-to-use-focus-peaking)

### How to use Focus Peaking

Desktop app

1.   Open an image in **Develop Studio**.
2.   Enable **Focus Peaking**  ![Image 8: Focus Peaking](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5MO22yxxotQ0LUieMia3K8/001c9db747c22fa865dd48af3611c1e2/focus_peaking.svg)       .
3.   Select an overlay color from the drop-down list.
4.   Inspect the image to identify highlighted areas of sharp focus.
5.   (Optional) Zoom in as needed to verify focus on key subjects.
6.   Continue editing once focus accuracy has been confirmed.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/focus-peaking/#see-also:)
