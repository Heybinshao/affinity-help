# Typography Studio

The Typography Studio provides essential tools and panels for controlling the formatting and positioning of text.

The Typography Studio is hidden by default. To show it, open the Studio Manager

![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7Hu0cvRLYlLOr92OfbxbF8/0890713fd47bb10637197141cdc62f77/studio_manager_help.svg)

 and turn on **Typography**. (You may need to scroll down to see it.)

After

Before

![Image 6: Layout after applying typgoraphy features.](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/48wy1qx6NaEwK90kfautuQ/43c7d98d570e5b9810ba91b336b67452/typography_after.jpg)

A page layout showing improved tracking and leading, a drop cap, a "T" graphic pinned to a heading, and OpenType ligatures and stylistic alternatives.

The **Paragraph** and **Character** panels let you apply local formatting to a whole story, a single character, or anything in between. They give you full control over alignment, kerning, leading, hyphenation, and other settings—and the Paragraph panel can also keep lone words or short lines from appearing at the top or bottom of text frames.

When you need a special character, turn to the **Glyph Browser** panel. The **Typography** panel gives you access to OpenType features, such as complex ligatures.

The **Text Styles** panel can turn formatting into reusable character and paragraph styles. Then apply the styles to other text in your document to keep things consistent and easy to update.

The **Pinning** panel helps objects stay with their related text. They can float beside the text or sit inline like characters.

Some changes you make in these panels, such as adding a drop cap, can affect your layout. To avoid switching between studios, the Typography Studio includes all the tools from the **Layout Studio**.
