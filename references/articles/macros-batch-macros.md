# Macros - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/macros-batch-macros/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Photo editing](https://www.affinity.studio/help/photo-editing/)
3.   Macros

Affinity includes a macro recording and playback feature, available via the Macro panel. It allows you to record multiple operations and then play them back sequentially as one action.

Through the **Macro** panel, you can record a wide range of operations in Affinity; from applying a Gaussian Blur to adding a gradient fill. It is hugely useful for faster workflows as you can record common editing steps and apply them to your projects in a single click. Additionally, you can exclude operations from the recording (if they were performed accidentally or are no longer needed) and expose certain parameters to the user for configurable playback.

Macros can be saved locally (they appear in the **Library** panel) and exported to an **.afmacro** file format for sharing and re-importing.

1.   On the **Macro** panel, click **Start recording**![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1cwdJYIzfPu5VTNjjDN7hL/fd4a2523a8279d494854eb71ba15848a/record_macro.png) .
2.   Perform the operations you wish to record. They will appear one by one in the macro operation list.
3.   (Optional) Uncheck any operations you do not wish to include (e.g., if they are no longer required).
4.   When you are finished recording, click **Stop recording**![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/22USxQXzqAUqCt8dMbf6Cw/f6c5c9beea315ba9f9971aca9c3088f0/stop_recording_macro.png) .
5.   (Optional) To continue recording the macro, click the **Start recording** icon again.
6.   (Optional) To play back and preview your macro, click **Play**![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3eqtj1FgaIwedY6Nz8loNJ/b2e93f0292fd54ed4751952d94df5bfb/playback_macro.png) .
7.   (Optional) To retrospectively change parameters on a particular operation, click the **Settings** icon next to the operation. This is useful for fine-tuning your macros, or quickly creating variations (e.g., Moderate, Standard, Extreme, etc.).
8.   (Optional) Within the **Settings** dialog for a recorded action, enable the **Eye** icon to expose the parameter to the user (making it interactive) when the macro is run.

1.   Once you have recorded your macro, click **Add to Library**![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7p1Dg588fVYcfCEarOJ7o7/6402dfeeb62608da34ec6c5af6129f12/AddMacroToLibrary.svg) .
2.   You will be prompted to choose a category and enter a name for your recorded macro. Enter a name and click **OK**.

The saved macro will appear in its category in the **Library** panel.

*   On the **Library** panel's **More** menu, select **Import Macros**.

*   With one or more operations listed, click **Reset**![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3AFNVriMaPCBIxtmGAPzDE/ce950e27ab66766ee7cae04c8286691d/reset_macro.svg) .

1.   Once you have recorded your macro, click **Export**![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5CVvlRmozd7kkxSpn7z3D9/194b5cd0ab3e4691935ad70040fa32c0/ExportMacro.svg) .
2.   In the file export dialog, choose a location and name for your macro, then click **Save**.
