# Paragraph panel - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/panels-paragraph-panel/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Design fundamentals](https://www.affinity.studio/help/design-fundamentals/)
3.   Paragraph panel

The Paragraph panel allows you to control the position and flow of individual paragraphs or entire stories.

For Mac/Windows: On the **Window** menu, select **Text > Paragraph**.

The **Paragraph** panel lets you:

*   Control the amount of vertical space between paragraphs.
*   Apply left and right indentations as well as indents for first lines only.
*   Create multiple tab stops set at different distances—lets you insert irregular spacing using the **⇥** key (Mac) / **Tab** key (Windows) several times.
*   Adjust how justified text behaves—lets you control how Affinity spaces letters and words so the text is flush with both margins.

The following options are available on the panel:

*   ![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3Yg1cAkoCjBhZXnTWr9CEy/1b6042b3e9036f6b2bac6c8e4bd9df90/Left_Align.svg) **Left Align**—sets the paragraph alignment to adhere to the left margin.
*   ![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7DgY2h36jUExYrknd04NOI/f6a97d0eeb0a00ca2c0d583ff0ea31f2/Centre_Align.svg) **Center Align**—sets the paragraph alignment to be equidistant from the left and right margins.
*   ![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3GXvijUk3QLtkbULoPJ3gM/e547ce40491e7fce92db645db050d6c9/Right_Align.svg) **Right Align**—sets the paragraph alignment to adhere to the right margin.
*   ![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/26uR5fox6QLP7Kq8nI1TYS/94651ece3a437d40291b5f00680b9160/Justified_Left.svg) **Justified Left**—sets the paragraph alignment to both the left and right margins. The last line of a paragraph is left aligned.
*   ![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1QGiMGDfSCZSuoSvxFHbrD/5d78801c9531ef29361ba51f08a0feb2/Justified_Centre.svg) **Justified Center**—as with Justified Left, except the last line of a paragraph is center aligned.
*   ![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4EZrpWEwFXFpF2JAzkVxx/e2e6a20c7c772e65a10704804bf2d17c/Justified_Right.svg) **Justified Right**—as with Justified Left, except the last line of a paragraph is right aligned.
*   ![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6x2A1VvmSdRXw02xIYd045/baba075519fa0055b6bf745c14396d11/Justified_All.svg) **Justified All**—as with Justified Left, except the last line of a paragraph is justified regardless of length (sometimes known as Force-Justified).
*   ![Image 8](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1ex8jwXDh7jNxPfGJvi8LT/ec4d00bcf85db1e6173140c709421217/towards-spine.svg) **Align Towards Spine**—aligns the paragraph dynamically towards the left or right of the spine depending on relative positioning of the text frame in relation to the spine (no justification used).
*   ![Image 9](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3u7tacSg43ELeT8N7U1MCb/162002065f04c7dd365d8b3b2ccf5c88/away_from_spine.svg) **Align Away From Spine**—aligns the paragraph dynamically away from the left or right of the spine depending on relative positioning of the text frame in relation to the spine (no justification used).
*   **Text Style**—applies a paragraph [text style](https://www.affinity.studio/help/text-text-styles/) to the selected text.

*   **Leading**—sets the distance between text baselines (vertical gap between lines) within the paragraph. Options on the pop-up menu include: 
    *   **Default**—sets the line spacing to the font's default (i.e., single).
    *   **Exactly**—sets a fixed spacing (other text attributes are ignored for line spacing purposes).
    *   **% Height**—sets spacing based on a percentage of the text's size.
    *   **At Least**—sets a minimum spacing (actual line spacing may increase depending on other text attributes).
    *   **Multiple**—controls line spacing as a portion of the default.

 When a value other than _Default_ is selected, you can choose from a range of preset values on the pop-up menu. 
*   ![Image 10](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2IOBA75xzdETWyDtVDPWmc/640aea6ecbbfbd610c68a239c4cad990/indent_left.svg) **Left Indent**—sets the left indent applied to the entire paragraph (excluding the first line).
*   ![Image 11](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2y6G0fVQ4IU0hO7tOavZDO/dc4d09a2313ce7d4e33774c67d8f39ba/indent_right.svg) **Right Indent**—sets the right indent applied to the entire paragraph.
*   ![Image 12](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1hk6BIUndkcTsPFhWeUnfW/ed83a9a4f4ca36115701f30a8b46aae4/before_paragraph.svg) **Space Before**—sets the vertical gap which precedes the paragraph. By default, this is not applied at the top of a column; this can be altered from the **Use space before:** pop-up menu.
*   ![Image 13](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/61NWGJL1vdHuU2wMidB5dr/ed97806e24b37628058214f09e336f89/indent_firstline.svg) **First Line Indent**—sets the indent applied to the first line of the paragraph.
*   ![Image 14](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4RHGZWw7iSlY46eQnnmZ7Q/4c1a5ff706c1e0bf74a05b80c8239cb4/outdent_lastline.svg) **Last Line Outdent**—sets the outdent applied to the last line of the paragraph.
*   ![Image 15](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6iuJTV0HIbtLrYhCc2wspw/9f584534ff31da0fd4fc39fdd6866f7a/after_paragraph.svg) **Space After**—sets the vertical gap which follows the paragraph.
*   **Space between same styles**—when this checkbox is checked, you can manually set the spacing between paragraphs of the same style.
*   **Sum space before and after**—when this checkbox is checked, the sum of the **Space Before Paragraph** and the **Space After Paragraph** settings are used to determine spacing between paragraphs.
*   **Use space before**—controls when **Space Before Paragraph** settings are applied to the text. Select from the pop-up menu.
*   **Align to Baseline Grid**—when this checkbox is checked, paragraph text is automatically aligned to the Baseline Grid.
*   **Use modern leading calculation**—uses metrics provided by the applied font to calculate leading instead of a fixed value. Enabled by default for new documents; disabled by default when opening documents created in older Affinity apps.

By default, the total space between two paragraphs is the larger of the **Space After** value and the **Space Before** values.

*   **Default Spacing**—controls the standard horizontal space added before a character when a tab is inserted using the **⇥** key (Mac) / **Tab** key (Windows). 
*   ![Image 16](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5Bi5j5RpX8dhsHuPx6HxCY/a853755c830ec5c50dd480eb8c4d2ca1/add-new-tab-stop.svg) **Add New Tab Stop**—adds an additional tab stop position.
*   **Tab stop alignment**—sets the alignment of the selected tab stop to _Left_, _Center_, _Right_, or _Decimal_.
*   ![Image 17](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1HMTJisSdk97KCF7UAQZ2T/af626b5eb59560a8c7119c9dbc1334f8/leader_none.svg) ![Image 18](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3kLCxIylYKfsWmtZ5s7Qbu/7d21904a914298de4ecd94b9a68d652e/leader_glyph.svg) ![Image 19](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/41SEgMRSMr33O4N7LmlKOf/7f5930ea877e48f1c55c1b7a66e4ca41/leader_underline.svg) ![Image 20](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2ClXVxC4kojwy6Xe6WQyuk/54a75023ea600ca04fdb49160383b029/leader_strikeout.svg) **Tab stop leader**—sets the characters shown to indicate the selected tab stop.
*   ![Image 21](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5GKMygAHyredYA3sp9qhwr/e6daff47a6c427bfb5491c9043a8be72/clear_x.png) **Delete**—removes the corresponding tab stop.

*   ![Image 22](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2IV164Lc8jgyWfJzkbLcF4/41e14b81b1ffee3b4b18f5c826bf9dde/word_spacing_min.svg) **Minimum Word Spacing**—sets the minimum gap allowed.
*   ![Image 23](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/78yTb98RV4UcATWJ20vqqD/ac38ed9a63919212b18f3c6f2f407884/word_spacing_desired.svg) **Desired Word Spacing**—sets the preferred gap between words.
*   ![Image 24](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/10i6gYcFOmhbnazqClHoHJ/1a6ba69677b2d60f33abc5cb373087f1/word_spacing_max.svg) **Maximum Word Spacing**—sets the maximum gap allowed.
*   ![Image 25](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6rmeDQ6JPhwe39oTEwRIUR/9c58036a0c5e80c2ad120ca4dadb3a9c/letter_spacing_min.svg) **Minimum Letter Spacing**—sets the minimum tracking allowed.
*   ![Image 26](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2JlyrGDvamnCb69juGex5w/df8a0e455be6dd2b721ca7e26997c226/letter_spacing_desired.svg) **Desired Letter Spacing**—sets the preferred tracking (spacing between letters) within words when a paragraph has a justified alignment.
*   ![Image 27](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1EbZFc9jKigRSNlB93fFba/f483f72c13927e6dec28d4d6ce03ab13/letter_spacing_max.svg) **Maximum Letter Spacing**—sets the maximum tracking allowed.

*   **Start**—determines where a paragraph should begin. Select from the pop-up menu: 
    *   **Anywhere**—fits the paragraph using default settings.
    *   **In Next Column**—fits the paragraph in the next column.
    *   **In Next Frame**—fits the paragraph in the next frame.
    *   **On Next Page**—fits the paragraph on the next page.
    *   **On Next Odd Page**—fits the paragraph on the next odd page.
    *   **On Next Even Page**—fits the paragraph on the next even page.

*   **Keep with previous paragraph**—ensures the paragraph is kept with the previous paragraph on the page or column.
*   **Prevent orphaned first lines**—ensures the first line of the paragraph is not separated from the rest of the paragraph at the bottom of a page or column.
*   **Keep paragraph together**—ensures that the paragraph is not split apart.
*   **Prevent widowed last lines**—ensures the last line of the paragraph is not separated from the rest of the paragraph at the top of a page or column.
*   **Keep with next**—allows you to specify the number of lines that must be kept together.
*   **Line break mode**—controls how Affinity determines where line breaks can occur in your text. The following options are available: 
    *   _Auto_—behavior is based on the spelling language set on the **Character** panel. If that language breaks lines at spaces and hyphens, behavior is the same as the Western setting. Otherwise, it's the same as the International setting.
    *   _Western_—allows line breaks to occur at spaces and hyphens.
    *   _International_—uses language-specific rules to determine where lines can break. Useful for languages like Korean, Japanese, and Chinese.

*   **Type**—select a type of bullet or numbering list from the pop-up menu.
*   **Level**—enter a list level.
*   **Text**—allows you to adjust the symbol(s) you wish to use for your list, as well as the spacing between the symbols and the text. Click on the downward arrow or the **More** button to browse additional symbols.
*   ![Image 28: Glyph Browser](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5lYHROhA1Dz48uOGVY7nvh/05a78ac1ec799c29df504f84d7f1692e/glyphs_browser.svg) **Choose a font glyph**—lets you select a glyph from the current font to use as a bullet.
*   ![Image 29: Image bullet](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3nej209mcJd2ClNPCg1nSD/d373523cdeba6ebfd96ddbb7b92800fd/image_bullet_list.svg) **Select an image file to make a custom bullet**—lets you select an image file to use as a custom bullet. The following options are available for adjusting the image bullet:
    *   **Scale**—sets the size of the image bullet relative to the font size. The image scales around its center-left anchor point.
    *   **Offset**—sets the vertical position of the image bullet relative to the font size.

*   **Tab stop**—adjust the distance text moves by in your list when the tab key is pressed.
*   **Alignment**—select from ![Image 30](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3Yg1cAkoCjBhZXnTWr9CEy/1b6042b3e9036f6b2bac6c8e4bd9df90/Left_Align.svg) **Left Align Bullet/Number**, ![Image 31](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7DgY2h36jUExYrknd04NOI/f6a97d0eeb0a00ca2c0d583ff0ea31f2/Centre_Align.svg) **Center Align Bullet/Number**, or ![Image 32](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3GXvijUk3QLtkbULoPJ3gM/e547ce40491e7fce92db645db050d6c9/Right_Align.svg) **Right Align Bullet/Number**. For the latter two options, **First Line Indent** must be greater than 0.
*   **Start numbering at**—for numbered lists, you can adjust the number the list starts from.
*   **Restart numbering**—for numbered lists, you can restart the numbering at certain points within the list.
*   **Restart numbering now**—tick this checkbox to immediately restart the numbering from the current point within your list.
*   **Name**—name your list.
*   **Global**—tick this checkbox to make this list available to be used multiple times within your design.
*   **Style**—select a style from the pop-up menu or click **New** to create a new style using the pop-up menu.

*   **Use auto-hyphenation**—tick this checkbox to enable automatic hyphenation options to be used.
*   **Minimum score**—specify the minimum possible hyphenation score. Setting this score to a higher number will result in fewer words being automatically hyphenated.
*   **Minimum word length**—specify the minimum number of characters for hyphenated words.
*   **Minimum prefix**—specify the minimum number of characters to be used for a prefix to be hyphenated.
*   **Minimum suffix**—specify the minimum number of characters to be used for a suffix to be hyphenated.
*   **Max consecutive hyphens**—specify the maximum number of consecutive hyphens.
*   **Hyphenation zone**—specify the amount of space allowed before hyphenation begins.
*   **Capital zone**—specify the amount of space allowed before hyphenation begins where words are in all capitals.
*   **Paragraph end zone**—specify the amount of space allowed at the end of a paragraph before hyphenation begins.
*   **Column end zone**—specify the amount of space allowed at the end of a column before hyphenation begins.

Auto-hyphenation has no effect on words that contain a soft hyphen. Putting a soft hyphen at the beginning of a word will prevent it from being hyphenated altogether.

*   **Enabled**—tick this checkbox to enable drop caps.
*   **Height in lines**—specify the height of the dropped capital in lines.
*   **Characters**—specify the number of characters to be formatted as drop caps.
*   **Auto**—when unchecked, use the Characters setting to specify how many characters are formatted as drop caps, regardless of their types. When checked, the drop cap encompasses the first alphanumeric character and any preceding punctuation.
*   **Distance to text**—specify how close the dropped capital and the rest of the text should be. This value can be negative to allow the text to be closer to the dropped capital.
*   **Align left edge**—tick this checkbox to ensure the dropped capital is aligned to the left-hand edge of the column.
*   **Scale for descenders**—tick this checkbox to ensure that the size of any dropped capital containing descenders is automatically adjusted so it matches the alignment of other dropped capitals.
*   **Style**—select a style from the pop-up menu or click **New** to create a new style using the pop-up menu.

*   **Enabled**—tick this checkbox to enable initial word formatting to be used.
*   **Max word count**—specify the maximum number of words to which initial word formatting should be applied.
*   **End characters**—specify which characters can be used to automatically end the initial word formatting.
*   **Style**—select a style from the pop-up menu or click **New** to create a new style using the pop-up menu.

*   **Decoration**—select a decoration to apply to the selected paragraph. Use the plus and minus buttons to create a new decoration or delete an existing decoration.
*   ![Image 33](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4vYUzrWgb3oOuu3gP3tbZj/e4b86ea26e69b1e64861b4e7916cbadf/decoration_left.svg) ![Image 34](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1rrw8VkM3jovvNIiahYPrN/8990d2985e253a494a689fbe9afd67f8/decoration_top.svg) ![Image 35](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3niC4H2KpyrpilT8Ch9XFw/778d93fc7f356fa0da8f23df77a3749c/decoration_right.svg) ![Image 36](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/58q525qNdUvyv6YJf5DCxy/23c421142f2e85a623d422022fb2a73b/decoration_bottom.svg) ![Image 37](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/TtX6aP9xV46Zgn1fkFGnE/552cca252f295a7b6bfc6cd4e8dc6d0b/decoration_fill.svg) **Position**—specify where you would like the decoration to appear in relation to the selected paragraph. Select from **Left**, **Top**, **Right**, **Bottom**, or **Fill** (additional options will become available on the panel when one of these is selected).
*   **Indent**/**Relative to**—adjust the settings to position the decoration to your liking. A positive **Indent** position will move the decoration closer to the text, whereas a negative **Indent** position will move a decoration further away from the text.
*   **Stroke**—click the color swatch to display a pop-up panel to update stroke color.
*   **Decoration stroke style**—set the stroke style, width, joins, cap ends, order and arrowhead settings via a pop-up panel.
*   **Fill**—click the color swatch to display a pop-up panel to update fill color.
*   **Transparency**—click the swatch to display a pop-up panel. See the [Gradient fills](https://www.affinity.studio/help/clr-gradient-editor/) topic for more information on the settings available.
*   **Combine identical**—tick this checkbox to automatically combine identical decorations.

 The following options are available on the **Panel Preferences** menu:

*   **Panels**—opens a dialog where you can quickly set the visibility of all panels in the current Studio.
*   **Close**—hides the current panel.
*   **Close Panel Group**—hides the current panel and any others grouped with it.
