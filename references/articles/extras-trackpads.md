3.   Using a trackpad (Mac only)

Affinity products are designed to be used with multitouch trackpads, including those with Force Touch pressure sensitivity (Magic Trackpad 2 and built into recent MacBooks).

This feature is only available in Affinity for desktop.

The following gestures are available in Affinity when using a trackpad:

Trackpad gesture for rotating the canvas about the current cursor position

1.   In macOS's System Settings, click **Trackpad**.
2.   Select **Point & Click** or **Scroll & Zoom** as appropriate.
3.   Check or uncheck the box next to the relevant gesture.

Disabling a multitouch gesture is system-wide, not just for Affinity.

In Affinity's **Settings**:

1.   Select **Tools**.
2.   Check or uncheck **Enable canvas rotation with trackpad**.
3.   In macOS's System Settings app: 
    1.   Click **Trackpad**.
    2.   Select **Scroll & Zoom**.
    3.   Check or uncheck **Rotate** to match your choice in Affinity.

The canvas's orientation can be reset by selecting **View > Rotate > Reset**.

Force Touch can be used as an alternative to **^(ctrl)**-clicking or using macOS's Secondary click trackpad gesture (if enabled).

1.   Select Affinity's **Settings**.
2.   Select **Tools**.
3.   Check or uncheck **Force Touch context menu** as required.
