# Hyperlinks panel - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/panels-hyperlinks-panel/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Design fundamentals](https://www.affinity.studio/help/design-fundamentals/)
3.   Hyperlinks panel

The Hyperlinks panel allows you to add, view and edit hyperlinks and their properties from your document. When you edit a hyperlink on the panel, it updates automatically on the page.

For Mac/Windows: On the **Window** menu, select **References > Hyperlinks**.

The panel allows you to work with hyperlinks from across your whole document or selected pages or spreads.

Exported PDF files with compatibility set to PDF1.x automatically include your hyperlinks.

Any table of contents or index that is generated will automatically create hyperlinks to page references.

The following options are available on the panel:

*   **Area**—sets the area whose hyperlinks to show to either **All Spreads** or a specific spread.

*   **Name**—displays the name(s) of the displayed hyperlink(s). This list can be sorted using the up and down arrows.
*   ![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6C90BouHwFxFl82bZZvk3/696f828f5efc492b08ef10070576a51c/hyperlink_source.svg) **Go to Source**—when clicked, navigates to the hyperlink's source location.

*   ![Image 11](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3m8ciDtLqf07Yrma1i0j4x/202c1d508104bbb0a1290c5be7725eae/trash_can.svg) **Remove Hyperlink**—removes the currently selected hyperlink from your document.

![Image 12](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6xHYQYa3ePyLnNbDC3c5Ey/56d26d75e40a2d195e578a41b2b16612/panel_preferences.svg)

 The following options are available on the **Panel Preferences** menu:

*   **Panels**—opens a dialog where you can quickly set the visibility of all panels in the current Studio.
*   **Close**—hides the current panel.
*   **Close Panel Group**—hides the current panel and any others grouped with it.
