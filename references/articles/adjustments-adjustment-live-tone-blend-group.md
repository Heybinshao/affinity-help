3.   Live Tone Blend Group adjustment

The Live Tone Blend Group automatically blends its contained layers with the underlying content, helping elements integrate smoothly into a composite and simplifying complex blending workflows.

![Image 1: After](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1gyY8OGdigmDfK0cG7AKCk/2e63e3e491f4d9c1269c5ff950c405fd/live-tone-blend-group_after.jpg)

After applying the Live Tone Blend Group to harmonize colors and tones.

The Live Tone Blend Group is a special group that automatically blends any layers placed inside it with the combined layer content beneath. It’s designed to simplify compositing by helping tones and colors integrate naturally across multiple layers. With built-in, user-configurable settings, you can fine-tune the blending result directly on the group, reducing the need for clipped adjustments or complex layer setups.

The following settings can be adjusted in the dialog:

*   **Blend Strength**—controls how strongly the group blends with the underlying layers. Higher values increase the influence of the composite beneath the group, while lower values produce a subtler blending effect.
*   **Blend Color**—adjusts the color intensity of the blended result. If the blend appears overly saturated, reduce this value to gradually desaturate the output while keeping tonal blending intact.
*   **Low Pass**—controls the amount of blur applied during blending. In most cases, leaving this at the maximum value produces the smoothest results. For text or vector layers, lowering the value can preserve more detail from the underlying composite.
*   **Content Type**—optimises the blending behaviour for different layer types. Image, vector, and text content each use blending techniques tailored to their characteristics. The content type is set automatically based on the first layer added to the group, but you can override it to experiment with alternative blending results.

1.   On the **Layers**panel, select the layers you would like to include in the blend process.
2.   Access the **Live Tone Blend Group** using one of the following methods:
    1.   on the **Pixel** menu, select **Pixel > Live Tone Blend Group**
    2.   right-click on the page and select **Pixel > Live Tone Blend Group**
    3.   in the Compositing Studio, on the top bar, click **Pixel > Live Tone Blend Group**![Image 2: Live Tone Blend Group](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4QwnwbtLzNdMFmosyTP9zQ/eba11ef9ff6694eff2139b714bc5cdd7/live_tone_blend_group.svg) 

3.   Edit the on-dialog options to achieve a desired effect.
4.   Close the dialog.

1.   On the **Layers**panel, locate the Live Tone Blend Group with layers affected.
2.   Click the group's icon to access the dialog.
3.   Adjust the Live Tone Blend Group parameters.
4.   Close the dialog.
