# Canva AI and Affinity Machine Learning (ML) - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-affinity-ml/

Canva and Affinity’s intelligent technologies open new possibilities for fulfilling your creative potential. Depending on your workflow, licensing needs, or personal preferences, you can choose between two options: the server-sided Canva AI or on-device Affinity Machine Learning models.

The Canva AI Studio in Affinity provides powerful, purpose-built tools and features to complement your workflow.

Here's what's included so far:

*   [Remove Background](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-remove-background/)—automatically isolates the main subject by removing the background, making it easier to create cutouts and composites.
*   [Select Subject](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-select-subject/)—detects and selects the primary subject in an image, speeding up masking and targeted edits.
*   [Create Depth Map](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-create-depth-map/)—generates a depth map from an image to enable depth-based selections and effects.
*   [Generate Image/Vector](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-generate-image/)—creates new images or vector graphics from text prompts, supporting rapid concept development.
*   [Object Selection Tool](https://www.affinity.studio/help/tools-tools-object-selection/)—quickly selects objects or regions to isolate areas for editing or compositing.
*   [Generative Expand Tool](https://www.affinity.studio/help/tools-tools-generative-expand/)—extends the canvas by generating new content that blends seamlessly with the existing image.
*   [Generative Fill Tool](https://www.affinity.studio/help/tools-tools-generative-fill/)—fills selected areas with AI-generated content based on surrounding pixels and text prompts.
*   [Generative Edit Tool](https://www.affinity.studio/help/tools-tools-generative-edit/)—modifies images using prompts to add, remove, or transform elements without manual reconstruction.
*   [Portrait Blur Tool](https://www.affinity.studio/help/tools-tools-portrait-blur/)—applies depth-of-field blur to isolate subjects and simulate camera lens effects.
*   [Portrait Lighting Tool](https://www.affinity.studio/help/tools-tools-portrait-lighting/)—adds or adjusts light sources to enhance subject definition and mood.
*   [Colorize Tool](https://www.affinity.studio/help/tools-tools-colorize/)—applies color to grayscale or low-color images to restore or stylize appearance.
*   [Super Resolution Tool](https://www.affinity.studio/help/tools-tools-super-resolution/)—upscales images while preserving detail, improving clarity and print quality.
*   [Select Sampled Depth Tool](https://www.affinity.studio/help/tools-tools-select-sampled-depth/)—selects areas based on depth information to support depth-aware edits and effects.

No, your content in Affinity is not used to train AI-powered features. Nor is it used to help AI features learn and improve in other ways, such as model evaluation or quality assurance. In Affinity, your content is stored locally on your device and we don’t have access to it. However, for any content you upload or export to Canva, you’re in control. You can review and update your preferences any time in your Canva settings.

If you have a Canva premium plan, you’ll be able to use Canva AI tools and Machine Learning features in Affinity. The price depends on your [Canva plan](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/pricing/).

Your AI usage is pooled across Affinity and Canva premium AI tools and refreshes each month. You can use your balance however you like, from generating images and vectors in Affinity to creating videos or exploring new ideas in Canva.

You can check the AI usage tracker anytime from the [Billing](https://www.canva.com/settings/billing-and-teams) page in your Canva Settings.

For Premium, and Ultra AI tools, you will be notified you as you near and reach your allowance limit. For ways to increase your AI allowance, see the [How do I get more access?](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/help/ai-access/) below.

You’ll get a notification in Affinity as you approach your AI allowance limit, so you can plan ahead.

If you reach your limit for premium AI features, you’ll see short pauses between generations. Once it ends, you can continue by entering your prompt again.

Tips to maximize your AI allowance:

*   Check out Canva's guide on [writing better prompts to maximize your AI allowance](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/help/ai-prompting-tips/).
*   Track your AI balance to plan ahead. See [Understanding your AI allowance](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-access/) to learn how to check your AI balance.

If you’ve reached your limit, below are some of your options for getting more AI access.

*   **Canva Pro and Canva Teams**: Upgrade your plan for more AI access, or purchase AI Pass as an add-on to your subscription. If you hit your limit, you can keep using AI tools with with short pauses between tasks.
*   **Canva for Business**: Purchase AI Pass as an add-on to your subscription. If you hit your limit, you can keep using AI tools with short pauses between tasks.
*   **Enterprise and Canva Education**: If you hit your limit, you can keep using premium AI features with short pauses between tasks.
*   **Canva for Nonprofits**: Purchase AI Pass as an add-on to your subscription. If you hit your limit, you can keep using premium AI features with short pauses between tasks.

Otherwise, wait for your allowance to reset.
