3.   Using undo, redo and history

It's easy to revert changes to a file when you've either made a mistake or if you simply don't like the result.

Any edits you make to your document are stored during your current session. You can undo your edits or redo an edit which you have recently undone.

A document's history can be saved along with the document, so earlier edits can be returned to even if the document is closed and reopened.

If you undo back to a step in history and you continue editing from that step, you can reinstate your redo history that would otherwise be lost. This might be because you changed your mind or if you made an edit that accidentally removed your redo history. Two branches in the history are created:

*   a visible new branch containing any new edits.
*   a hidden branch storing the redo history.

Do one of the following:

*   On the **Edit** menu, click **Undo** to go back one step, or **Redo** to redo the step previously undone.
*   On the **History** panel, click one of the entries in the list to jump to that step.
*   On the **History** panel, drag the slider left to undo a change, right to redo a change.

Shortcuts:

*   Undo: **⌘Z** (Mac) / **Ctrl**+**Z** (Windows)
*   Redo: **⇧⌘Z** (Mac) / **Shift**+**Ctrl**+**Z** (Windows)

*   On the **History** panel, drag the slider to the farthest position to the left.

*   Click **Cycle future**![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2AVyEdE4mRO1vvFuCh7RKR/fc19d1c6903e5025cf1a691fe604ef57/cycle_future.svg)  at the history step where you want to reinstate your original redo history. This history is hidden 'within' the icon, and will be reinstated on clicking.

1.   On the **History** or **Snapshot** panel, click **Set undo brush source**![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2o691le3lFGxWujdpQyb2Q/eb79cf7909bb37f42921e6700b55c9a4/undo_brush_tool_on.svg)  on the history state or snapshot you want to paint back to.
2.   Begin painting with the Undo Brush Tool.

1.   On the **File** menu, select **Save History with Document**.
2.   In the **Saving With History** dialog, click **Yes** to accept the conditions discussed in the dialog.
3.   Save your document.

Saving your document's history with your document may significantly increase the size of your project file.

You can switch off the save history feature by repeating step 1 of the procedure above.

Related behaviors can be adjusted from the app's settings:

*   **Performance > Undo Limit** (desktop only). Drag to the left to reduce the number of stored changes, right to increase the number of stored changes.
