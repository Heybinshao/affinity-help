# Appearance panel - Affinity Help Center

3.   Appearance panel

Use the Appearance panel to apply multiple strokes, fills, or both to a selected object. You can drag and drop to change their order, edit stroke widths, and apply different blend modes to each stroke and fill to create eye-catching effects.

For Mac/Windows: On the **Window** menu, select **Vector > Appearance**.

Using the panel, you can:

*   Add and manage strokes and fills for the selected object.
*   Hide or show strokes and fills.
*   Choose the active stroke and fill to edit on the **Stroke** or **Color** panel.
*   Apply separate blending control for each stroke and fill.
*   Delete strokes and fills.

The following options are available on the panel:

*   **Stroke**—an entry showing stroke color, width and blend mode. The active stroke is denoted by a white dot to its left. This stroke will be affected by edits on the **Stroke** panel.
*   **Fill**—an entry showing fill color and blend mode. The active fill is denoted by a white dot to its left. This fill will be affected by edits on the **Color** panel.

 The following options are available on the **Panel Preferences** menu:

*   **Panels**—opens a dialog where you can quickly set the visibility of all panels in the current Studio.
*   **Close**—hides the current panel.
*   **Close Panel Group**—hides the current panel and any others grouped with it.
