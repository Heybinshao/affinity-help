# Sharpen filters - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/filters-sharpen-filters/

Filters are applied to your image from the **Pixel > Filters** menu (destructive) or **Layer** menu (non-destructive) and mostly include customizable settings alongside general options. Non-destructive filters are called live filters, and, once applied, they can be identified as both a filter and a specific filter type by using unique symbols.
