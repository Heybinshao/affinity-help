# Pan/Scroll the document view

When your document is zoomed-in, you can work on different areas of your document by using panning.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/design-aids-pan/#how-to-pan-a-document)

### How to pan a document

Desktop app

1.   Click the **View Tool**  ![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/16xclkZTOokIeWj5LpEvxi/915d082ebd3ac857b57212d382357078/view_tool.svg)       . On some Studios, this may be on the Zoom Tool flyout.
2.   Drag the zoomed-in document in relation to its view.

Temporarily activate the **View Tool** by using Hot key panning: press the **Spacebar**.

You can also pan the document view using your mouse wheel, scroll bars, or the Navigator panel. The panel also lets you save the document view's current zoom level and position. Multiple view points can be saved, and you can instantly navigate between them.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/design-aids-pan/#see-also:)
