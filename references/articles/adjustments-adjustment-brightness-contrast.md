The following settings can be adjusted in the dialog:

*   **Brightness**—controls the lightness/darkness of the composition. Drag the slider to the left to decrease brightness, drag to the right to increase it.
*   **Contrast**—controls the tonal range of the composition. Drag the slider to the left to decrease the contrast between dark and light areas, drag to the right to increase it.
*   **Linear**—by default, the adjustment prevents shadow and highlight clipping by modifying pixels relative to their original lightness value. When selected, this option modifies pixels using absolute values and clipping may occur.

*   On the **Layers** panel, click **Adjustments**![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4ZFx9I9M7MiUBPmytBqZrt/cad5a29a5e39b1b3faa42c5fa29402f3/add_adjustment_layer.svg)  and select **Brightness and Contrast**.
*   On the **Adjustment** panel, select **Brightness / Contrast**![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/585EbECVUPqAnHHd8YJVzO/22f568c05dbc5f7269562ece0343b792/brightness_contrast_adjustment_type.svg)  and then click one of the available presets.
*   On the **Pixel** menu, select **New Adjustment Layer > Brightness / Contrast**.

The **Adjustment** panel is not visible by default. To enable it, navigate to the top menu and select **Window > Pixel > Adjustment**.
