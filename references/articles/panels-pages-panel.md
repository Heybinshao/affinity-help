# Pages panel - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/panels-pages-panel/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Design fundamentals](https://www.affinity.studio/help/design-fundamentals/)
3.   Pages panel

Use the Pages panel to view, add, and edit master pages, publication pages, sections, and spreads.

For Mac/Windows: On the **Window** menu, select **Layout > Pages**. Alternatively, on the **page navigation bar** (lower left of Affinity's main window), click **Toggle Pages Panel**

![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7HnnZvUw66oZ6MV8HSG1qY/0ce6f1b9673593a7fb223263deb3ab84/pages_panel_toggle.svg)

.

The following options are available on the panel:

*   **Master Pages**—this window displays the master pages that are currently available in your document. These pages contain background elements that are typically shared by multiple pages. On the **Master Pages** window, you can do the following: 
    *   ![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6bju7KVEsaDa5JdQ5r9Klm/3942375af19024a82a791a7f116fcec2/add_master_pages.svg) **Add Master**—opens the **Add Master Page** dialog, allowing you to create an additional master page.
    *   ![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/RJpVOUJCTbGYUesRprC7s/cab464a26fbad0fa2eb00f3e300ecf32/duplicate_master_pages.svg) **Duplicate Selected Master**—duplicates the selected master page(s).
    *   ![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3m8ciDtLqf07Yrma1i0j4x/202c1d508104bbb0a1290c5be7725eae/trash_can.svg) **Delete Selected Master**—deletes the selected master page(s).

*   **Pages**—this window displays publication pages and spreads within your document. On the **Pages** window, you can do the following: 
    *   ![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2TDNSfgxFp4cONNjp2iTz1/13fb5495a1a4761675b891f48cec42d3/section_manager.svg) **Section Manager**—opens the **Section Manager** dialog, allowing you to add, delete and manage sections and adjust page numbering.
    *   ![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/40E05kD2SYXaUT8gELmImG/eab02af1b558ece3fa633a369815fb94/add_pages.svg) **Add Pages**—allows you to create additional pages.
    *   ![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7ycyMa2nOORQwgSNPxdEIZ/4688670bb049730cc2b1eb64d8d69cbd/duplicate_pages.svg) **Duplicate Selected Pages**—allows you to duplicate selected pages.
    *   ![Image 8](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3m8ciDtLqf07Yrma1i0j4x/202c1d508104bbb0a1290c5be7725eae/trash_can.svg) **Delete Selected Pages**—allows you to delete selected pages.

When icons are medium or large, they wrap to fit the panel width. You could, for example, position the panel on a secondary display and make it wider to present a flatplan-like overview of your document. When the icons are set to small, they are instead presented in a vertical list, regardless of the panel's width.

You can reveal more of the **Master Pages** window by dragging the darker **Pages** bar downwards.

![Image 9](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6UxpXBt5miovw347YaOPUz/6f8e3e8d9b379daa0bdc5a3ac6ff23e8/panel_preferences.svg)

 The following options are available on the **Panel Preferences** menu:

*   **Small**/**Medium**/**Large**/**Extra Large Thumbnails**—determines the size of the page thumbnails shown on the panel. (Extra large scales thumbnails to almost fill the panel's width.)
*   **Scroll With View**—when selected, the **Pages** panel will scroll automatically to match the currently active viewed page in your document.
*   **Show Master Page Tags**—when selected, any color tags you've assigned to master pages are displayed as matching colored lines above the thumbnails of any pages to which the master is applied.
*   **Page Move Options**—contains options for how moving a page affects its applied master and objects: 
    *   **Reflow Pages**—when selected, Affinity will attempt to maintain the correct number of pages in spreads.
    *   **Split Masters**—any publication page that is moved retains the page of its master for its previous position. For example, a left page that becomes a right page retains its master's left page, and vice versa.
    *   **Move Master Content**—any publication page that is moved has the page of its master for its new position applied to it. Modified inherited objects and local content are moved with the page. Unmodified inherited objects are replaced with the 'correct' master page content for the destination spread.
    *   **Reapply Masters**—any publication page that is moved has the page of its master for its new position applied to it. Local edits to frame content are always retained, but edited object attributes may be lost.
    *   **Anchor Toward Spine**—controls the positioning of objects when their page is moved across the spine. When selected, objects maintain their distance from the spine. When unselected, objects maintain their absolute position on the page.

*   **Panels**—opens a dialog where you can quickly set the visibility of all panels in the current Studio.
*   **Close**—hides the current panel.
*   **Close Panel Group**—hides the current panel and any others grouped with it.

*   To display a page or spread in the document view, double-click its thumbnail on the Pages panel.
*   To rename a master page, click on its name on the Pages panel, then click again.
*   To fit a spread to the document view and center it, double-click the page numbers below its thumbnail on the Pages panel.
