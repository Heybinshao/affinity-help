# About Canva Teams - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/about-canva-teams/

We’ve introduced [Canva Business](http://canva.com/canva-business), a new plan with extra tools to help individuals, marketers, and businesses grow.

While Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups or upgrades, **existing Canva Teams subscribers will keep their current plan** unless they choose to cancel or switch plans.

If you’re already on the Canva Teams plan, you’ll:

*   Keep your current pricing (including when adding new members)
*   Keep all the same features in your plan
*   Continue to get new premium features that enhance your design experience - the same features added to Canva Pro

However, you won't receive the features that come with Canva Business.

If you’re already on Canva Teams, your plan remains value-packed and a great fit. **Canva Business** is simply another option for those looking for more ways to grow their brand and business.

If you cancel your Canva Teams subscription, you won’t be able to resubscribe to Canva Teams, as it’s no longer available for new sign-ups or upgrades. You can choose from other plans, including Canva Pro or Canva Business, at any time.

If you upgraded to Canva Business from Canva Teams in error and want to return to your previous plan, get in touch [here](https://www.canva.com/help/get-in-touch/contact-support/?issue_category=canva-plans&issue=switch-from-business-to-teams).

**Canva Pro**is the perfect plan for solo creators who want premium editing tools and templates to create faster and design like a pro.

**Canva Business** is built for individuals and growing teams to power their business growth. It expands on what you get with Pro, plus higher AI access, stronger brand management, and smarter marketing with ad analytics to scale with confidence. Learn more about [Canva Business](https://www.canva.com/help/about-canva-business).

Check our [pricing page](https://www.canva.com/pricing/) to see the full list of features available for each plan.

If you’re an existing Canva Teams subscriber, you’ll stay on your Canva Teams subscription unless you choose to cancel or switch plans. You’ll keep your current pricing (including when adding new members) and features, and continue to get new premium features - the same added to Canva Pro.

To learn more about our plans, head to our [pricing page](http://www.canva.com/pricing/).
