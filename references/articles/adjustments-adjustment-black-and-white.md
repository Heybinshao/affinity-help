The Black & White adjustment

![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3PgOWd2qBooTcJXPAKxsk1/9209748380a579021b8067e31d47a007/black_and_white_adjustment_type.svg)

 allows you to transform color images into monochrome, providing complete control over how each individual color is represented in the final image.

![Image 2: Black & White after](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7KrHFZc32gyeTg5HrVV22X/534ca4037caf022b46785206939b0e06/adjustment_black_white_after.jpg)

![Image 3: Black & White before](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5NXgmzFzXKve50c596UktV/3587c26a6e00f462f4d50c450a098ea1/adjustment_base.jpg)

This adjustment comes with a handy **Picker** setting which allows you to target a color in the document and adjust the applicable slider.

You can also create black and white outputs using the **HSL**, **Gradient Map**, and **Threshold** adjustments.

You may wish to apply a color tone, such as sepia, to the resulting grayscale image. To do this, you could use a **Recolor**

![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1mXiV5KikL6J52aobotdQu/78e7842efbe23bf13c0f7f6609c704c4/recolour_adjustment_type.svg)

 adjustment.

The following settings can be adjusted in the dialog:

*   The sliders control the lightness value of the named color areas of the composition. Drag the slider to the left to darken areas of the named color, drag the slider to the right to lighten them.
*   **Picker**—allows you to drag on the page to modify the adjustment. The initial click will identify the predominant color, dragging left will darken areas of the identified color while dragging right will lighten them. The corresponding slider will be updated.

The **Picker** option is not available in the Develop Studio.

*   On the **Layers** panel, click **Adjustments**![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4ZFx9I9M7MiUBPmytBqZrt/cad5a29a5e39b1b3faa42c5fa29402f3/add_adjustment_layer.svg)  and select **Black and White**
*   On the **Adjustment** panel, select **Black & White**![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3PgOWd2qBooTcJXPAKxsk1/9209748380a579021b8067e31d47a007/black_and_white_adjustment_type.svg)  and then click one of the available presets.
*   On the **Pixel** menu, select **New Adjustment Layer > Black & White**.

This adjustment is also available in the Develop Studio on the **Tones** panel.

The **Adjustment** panel is not visible by default. To enable it, navigate to the top menu and select **Window > Pixel > Adjustment**.
