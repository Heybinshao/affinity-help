# Cancel a Canva plan - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/cancel-canva-plan/

2.   [Account and billing](https://www.affinity.studio/help/account-billing/)
3.   Cancel a Canva plan

Quick tip

Received an unfamiliar Canva payment? Look it up to learn what it's for.

**For Canva Teams**and**Canva Business**, only owners and administrators can cancel or pause plans.

**For users in South Korea,** if you’re on a 7-day free trial and don’t see a **Cancel****button**, that’s expected. Your trial will automatically end after 7 days, and your account will return to the **Free** plan without any charges. You don’t need to cancel it yourself.

The **Cancel****button** only appears if you’ve upgraded to a paid plan after your free trial.

If you've decided to cancel your Canva plan, follow the steps below.

It’s important to know which subscription type you have because the cancellation process is different for each one.

You have a **Canva-managed subscription** if you subscribed using a web browser (on computer or mobile), and received invoices directly from Canva.

You have an **app store subscription** if you subscribed through the Canva mobile app on your phone/tablet, your subscription appears in your device's app store subscriptions, and you're charged through your Apple ID or Google Play account.

You can cancel your Canva-managed subscription using any of these methods:

1.   Make sure to log in to the right Canva account or team.
2.   On the homepage, select your profile icon to open the menu.
3.   Select**Settings**.
4.   Go to **Payments and plans**and select**Billing.**
5.   Under your plan, select **Cancel plan.**
6.   Select **Continue cancellation**.
7.   Choose the reason why you want to cancel, then select **Continue cancellation**.
8.   (Optional) Tell us why you’re canceling.
9.   Select **Cancel subscription.**

**Important**: Uninstalling the app doesn't cancel your plan. You must cancel through your device's app store.

For Google Play subscription, you can cancel it from the [Google Play workflow](https://bit.ly/2G4eFJp), or follow these steps:

1.   On your device, open the **Play Store** app.
2.   On the top corner, select your **account icon**. Check if you’re signed in to the correct Google Account.
3.   Select **Payments & subscriptions**.
4.   Select **Subscriptions** to see your subscriptions.
5.   Select **Canva**, then **Cancel Subscription**.
6.   Confirm to cancel.

Learn more about [managing subscriptions on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid%26oco=1).

Quick tip

If you’ve cancelled your subscription and want to undo it, go to [**Billing**](https://www.canva.com/settings/billing-and-teams) page, then select **Stay on Canva Teams, Canva Business,**or**Canva Pro.**

This option is available only if you’ve requested cancellation and your subscription is still active.

Here are some things to check:

*   Check that you're logged into the correct account and team.
*   Check that your most recent payment was successful. If not, make sure to resolve that before moving forward. Our article [My payment failed or didn’t complete](https://www.canva.com/help/failed-payments) can help you get sorted.

Cancelling your subscription stops future payments, but it won't automatically issue a refund. Depending on your plan, you may still have access to paid features until the end of your billing period.

You’ll still be able to access Affinity for free with your Canva account, but any additional features from your plan will be removed when your subscription ends.

Canceling your plan doesn’t delete your existing designs and Brand Kit. You can use them when you upgrade to Canva Pro or Canva Business again.

See our [pricing page](https://www.canva.com/pricing/) to find out what's included in each plan.

To help us provide the best assistance, [tell us what went wrong](https://www.canva.com/help/get-in-touch/contact-support/?issue_category=payments-pricing-and-billing&issue=manage-pro-subscription&article=cancel-canva-plan&greeting=cancel-canva-plan) and provide a copy of your invoice. If you purchased your plan from the App Store or Google Play Store, [find your order number or receipt ID](https://www.canva.com/help/find-read-invoices) and send that instead.
