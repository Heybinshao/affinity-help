3.   Draw and edit geometric shapes

Geometric and other special shapes that would be otherwise hard to draw can be easily created using shape tools.

Once drawn, geometric shapes can be morphed into many shape variants directly on the page. For a more asymmetric look, any shape can be converted to curves for more freeform design.

![Image 1: Shape morphing example](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6P22HMCFZSXec08dekGuG2/a42e22415066e40ac3b3e609170d2391/shapes_edit.png)

1.   Choose one of the shape tools, e.g., **Rectangle Tool**![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4GgxISWf3JyAdu2jUP9AI2/17f7e780a2508f1344062338aec23a3a/rectangle_tool.svg) .
2.   Do one of the following: 
    *   For sizing **by eye**: Drag on the page, using the **⇧** key (Mac) / **Shift** key (Windows) to constrain the shape's proportions if needed.![Image 3: Drag shape](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4c6T9bKik9UrcsDJdOesjm/c241c05b720e319c011d4b2449d86ad2/shapes_dragShape2.png) 
    *   For precise **Object data entry**: **⌘**-click (Mac) / **Ctrl**-click (Windows) on the page, then enter your shape dimensions and properties into the dialog. To add the same shape again, double-click on the page while the **⌘** key (Mac) / **Ctrl** key (Windows) is still pressed.On the page/pasteboard, shapes will always be centered under the current cursor position, but can be positioned in relation to a repositioned anchor point on the dialog.With **Snapping** enabled, shapes can also be created and centered on a shape’s bounding box (including corner handles) by **⌘**-clicking (Mac) / **Ctrl**-clicking (Windows).

With either method, modify options as required, either by changing the values on the context toolbar or by dragging the on-object red handle(s) (if available).

![Image 4: Drag handle](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/58iAqoLCCwXKkFhSEY7GoN/0f0e38878e360e5302b740578b575bb1/shapes_dragNode2.png)

When you hover over a handle, a straight red guide line will appear to suggest the direction of drag needed to modify your shape.

Shapes can be converted to text frames via **Layer > Convert > to Text Frame**.

*   Double-click the red handle that has been previously moved.

1.   Click the **Node Tool**![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2Tyrn620F9iCYiF39DxVOc/9de008f998af69eafee088eb5a3c5210/node_tool.svg) .
2.   Select the shape, either by clicking the shape or by clicking the layer entry in the **Layers** panel.
3.   Edit the shape as required by either by directly dragging the red handle(s), or by changing the values on the context toolbar.

*   On the **Vector** menu, click **Convert to Curves**.

![Image 6: Convert to Curves](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5zlnwz1eI5VB12AqubDVz2/9df5938aaddb898fc28888e935aececa/shapes_convertToCurves.png)

As you create shapes, the following modifier keys can be used while the shape is being drawn:

*   The **⇧** key (Mac) / **Shift** key (Windows) constrains shape's proportions at the time of creation.
*   The **⌘** key (Mac) / **Ctrl** key (Windows) draws or resizes from center, and can be combined with the **⇧** key (Mac) / **Shift** key (Windows). 
*   For Mac: The **⌃(ctrl)** key lets you rotate a shape about its opposite handle.
*   For Windows: Pressing the right mouse button lets you rotate a shape about its opposite handle.
*   The **Spacebar** lets you reposition a shape at the time of creation.
