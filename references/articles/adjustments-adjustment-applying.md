3.   Applying adjustments

There are a range of adjustments which can be applied to your compositions as a new layer for creative or corrective purposes.

, adjustments can also be applied by:

*   painting with the **Adjustment Brush Tool**.
*   modifying the sliders on the **Quick Adjustments** panel.

To learn more, visit the dedicated topics.

There are additional adjustments available in the **Develop Studio**, which are arranged on various panels. For more information, see Developing a RAW image.

For specific targeting of adjustments, create a pixel selection before applying an adjustment layer. Only those pixels included in the selection are affected by the adjustment layer. (A layer mask is applied.) For more information, see the Adjustment layers topic.

The following general settings are available from all adjustment dialogs:

*   **Add Preset**—adds the current adjustment settings as a preset for use with later images and projects.
*   **Merge**—merges the current adjustment layer with the layer immediately below it in the layer order.
*   **Delete**—closes the dialog and deletes the adjustment layer, removing the adjustment from the composition.
*   **Reset**—reverts all dialog settings to default.
*   **Opacity**—how see-through the adjustment layer is.
*   **Blend mode**—changes how the applied pixels interact with existing pixels on the layer below. Choose mode type from a pop-up menu.
*   ![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5aLt8lzEcmovjtpJrbbZFI/521800938c5909fe2677c5c56dc42a30/settings_cog_layers.svg) **Blend Options**—click to access a dialog for setting the blend options, blend gamma and antialiasing settings for the selected layer.

You can reset any adjustment setting back to its default by double-clicking on its slider handle.

Not all adjustments have a dedicated dialog or customizable settings.

1.    On the **Layers** panel, do one of the following: 
    *   Select a layer to add the adjustment as a child of the layer. It will affect all objects on the selected layer.
    *   Select a layer, object, group or image to add the adjustment to the item, affecting just that item only.
    *   Deselect all items to add the adjustment at the top of the layer stack, applying the adjustment to all items below it (the entire spread).

2.   Click **Adjustments**![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4ZFx9I9M7MiUBPmytBqZrt/cad5a29a5e39b1b3faa42c5fa29402f3/add_adjustment_layer.svg)  and select an adjustment from the pop-up menu.
3.    If a dialog appears for the adjustment, follow the steps below: 
    1.   Adjust the settings in the dialog.
    2.   (Optional) Click **Add Preset** to make your custom settings available in step 2 for future sessions.
    3.   Close the dialog.

An **Adjustments** panel (**Window > Pixel > Adjustment**) can be used instead of using the **Layers** panel to apply adjustments.

To apply adjustments to content inside picture frames, ensure the content itself is selected before applying the adjustment.

Adjustments are also available on the **Pixel** menu's **New Adjustment Layer** entry.

1.   On the Layers panel, select an image layer.
2.   Open the **Quick Adjustments** panel.
3.   Drag any of the sliders to edit your image, as required.
4.   (Optional) At the bottom of the panel, click any of the auto adjustments, as required.

The Quick Adjustments panel is not on by default. You can open it from the **Window > General** menu.

You can quickly revert changes by selecting **Reset Adjustments** from the panel's Preferences menu. For more about Quick Adjustments, see the dedicated panel topic.

1.   On the **Layers** panel, double-click the adjustment layer that you want to modify.
2.   Adjust the settings in the dialog.
3.   Click **Close** to apply the changes, **Merge** to apply the changes and merge the adjustment with the layer beneath, or **Delete** to remove the adjustment layer entirely.

1.   On the **Layers** panel, tap the adjustment layer's icon to bring up its settings.
2.   Adjust the settings, as required.
3.   On the dialog, click **Add Preset**.
4.   Name the preset, then click **OK**.

The preset will be saved in the **Adjustments** panel along with other adjustments of that type.

1.   On the **Adjustment** panel, select an adjustment.
2.   Drag an adjustment preset thumbnail to move it to a different position.
