# Retouching Studio - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/workspace-retouching-studio/

The Retouching Studio is your complete workspace for enhancing and perfecting photos with precision and control.

Designed for both quick edits and in-depth adjustments, it brings together all the essential tools you need to refine color, tone, detail, and structure within your photos. Whether you’re making subtle touch-ups or performing complex edits across multiple layers, the Retouching Studio gives you a flexible, non-destructive environment to craft your perfect image while keeping your creative workflow seamless and efficient.

Photo retouching examples.

Inside the Retouching Studio, you’ll find a set of panels and tools that give you full control over every part of your edit.

Use the **Library** to browse and apply your pre-recorded actions, fine-tune them with **Adjustments**, and explore creative possibilities with **Brushes** and **Layers**. Panels like **color**, **Channels**, **Scope**, and **Histogram** help you balance tones and bring out depth and detail.

You can also streamline your process with **Macros**, revisit changes through **History** or **Snapshots**, and view important **Metadata**and **Sources** to keep your edits organised.

You can also organise your cloning **Sources** in the dedicated panel. Together, these panels make it easy to refine your images with confidence and consistency.
