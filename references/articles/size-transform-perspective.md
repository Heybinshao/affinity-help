3.   Perspective

Manipulating perspective helps you align elements realistically, control depth, and make designs feel grounded in the real world—whether you’re editing photos, building layouts, or creating mockups.

Perspective tools let you correct or reshape how an image appears in space. This is useful when:

*   Correcting tilted or distorted photos
*   Placing designs onto real-world surfaces
*   Creating realistic product mockups
*   Aligning artwork to match camera angles
*   Adding depth and realism to composites

Perspective editing is especially valuable when working with placed images, such as logos, posters, screens, packaging, or signage.

In Affinity, you can adjust perspective using:

*   The **Perspective Tool**(direct, hands-on editing)
*   The **Live Perspective Filter**(non-destructive, editable at any time)
*   The **Perspective Filter**(destructive filter)

Both support **Source** and **Destination** modes, which are key to advanced workflows and mockup design.

Source mode defines the area of the original image that will be transformed. You use it to:

*   Select the part of an image you want to remap
*   Define the original shape or plane
*   Isolate a design or texture before placing it

Destination mode defines _where_ and _how_ the source content will be mapped. You use it to:

*   Match perspective to a surface in a photo
*   Align content to walls, screens, packaging, or signs
*   Control angle, depth, and scale

Together, Source and Destination modes let you precisely remap content from one plane to another—ideal for mockups and compositing.

Placed images are especially powerful when combined with perspective tools. They are used because they stay linked to the original file, can be scaled and transformed cleanly, with the ability to update designs without reworking the mockup. The placed images workflows are ideal for logos, posters, UI screens, packaging, and branding.

1.   Select the layer you want to transform.
2.   Select the **Perspective Tool**![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7xK4dYaJUizyYD8ohI86nr/dac978ad72d99ec82f600d17baa4baab/perspective_tool.svg)  or add the **Live Perspective Filter**.
3.   Use Source mode to define the original area.
4.   Switch to Destination mode to place and align the content.
5.   Adjust the corners and edges until the perspective matches the scene.
6.   (Optional) Fine-tune with opacity, blend modes, or masks if needed.

Use the **⇧** key (Mac) / **Shift** key (Windows) to snap a grid handle to vertical or horizontal.

With **Snapping** enabled, the grid handles will snap to any page edge.

1.   **Place**your design (File > Place) into the document.
2.   Position it roughly over the target surface.
3.   Apply the **Perspective Tool**or **Live Perspective Filter**.
4.   In **Source**mode, define the full design area.
5.   In **Destination**mode, match the surface perspective.
6.   Use blend modes (Multiply, Overlay, Soft Light) or layer Opacity to integrate it naturally.
7.   (Optional) Add shadows or highlights on separate layers, if needed.
