# Creating books - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/advanced-creating-books/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Page layout](https://www.affinity.studio/help/page-layout/)
3.   Creating books

Use the Books panel to create and manage books. You can add, remove, replace, and reorder chapters there.

This feature is only available in Affinity for desktop.

If you add a document from in an older version of Affinity to a book, you'll see a warning. Continuing will update the file and make it incompatible with older versions. To keep compatibility with older versions, make a copy first. Add the copy to your book, not the original.

Each document you add can be a literal chapter, but this isn't required. You can divide content however works best for you. This may be influenced by your production workflow.

Multiple chapters can be simultaneously added or removed. One chapter at a time can be replaced or rearranged.

Multiple selected chapters can be opened at once:

*   For Mac: Double-click one of the selection.
*    For Windows: **Right**-click one of the selection, then click **Open**. 

Replacing chapters can help in a collaborative workflow, e.g. upon receiving a version that has been copy-edited by a colleague.

Adding a document as a chapter automatically modifies its file—for example, to fit the pagination of the book. If the same document is used in more than one book, it's recommended you use a copy in each to avoid conflicts between projects.

In books with facing-page spreads, single pages between chapters are called “stray” pages. Affinity offers two ways to handle them.

If a chapter ends on a left page and the next one starts on a right page, Affinity can merge them. This creates a single facing-page spread.

Where only one stray page exists, Affinity can insert pages to “pad” the document to maintain facing-page spreads.

*   On the **Books** panel, either: 
    *   Click **Create new book**. (Available if no books are open).
    *   On the **Panel Preferences** menu ![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6xHYQYa3ePyLnNbDC3c5Ey/56d26d75e40a2d195e578a41b2b16612/panel_preferences.svg) , select **New Book**.

1.   On the **Books** panel's **Panel Preferences** menu ![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6xHYQYa3ePyLnNbDC3c5Ey/56d26d75e40a2d195e578a41b2b16612/panel_preferences.svg) , select **Save Book**.
2.   On the dialog that appears: 
    1.   Type a name for your .afbook file.
    2.   Navigate to the required location for the file.
    3.   Click **Save**.

1.   On the **Books** panel, click **Add Chapter**![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/557PZwUf79x4CFvaBlDeZX/df4c79bc23760f650341a53295711e7e/add_chapter.svg) .
2.   Navigate to and select one or more Affinity documents (saved with the .af file extension).
3.   Click **Open**.

Alternatively, drag one or more Affinity (.af) documents onto the **Books** panel.

On the **Books** panel:

1.   Select one or more chapters.
2.   Click **Remove Chapter**![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3m8ciDtLqf07Yrma1i0j4x/202c1d508104bbb0a1290c5be7725eae/trash_can.svg) .

On the **Books** panel:

1.   Select a chapter.
2.   On the **Panel Preferences** menu ![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6xHYQYa3ePyLnNbDC3c5Ey/56d26d75e40a2d195e578a41b2b16612/panel_preferences.svg) , select **Replace Chapter**.
3.   Navigate to and select an Affinity (.af) document.
4.   Click **Open**.

On the **Books** panel:

1.   Drag a chapter up or down the list.
2.   When you see a blue line at the desired position, drop the chapter.

On the **Books** panel:

1.   On the **Panel Preferences** menu ![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6xHYQYa3ePyLnNbDC3c5Ey/56d26d75e40a2d195e578a41b2b16612/panel_preferences.svg) , make sure **Stray Pages > Merge Where Possible** is checked. If it isn't already, select it.

On the **Books** panel:

1.   On the **Panel Preferences** menu ![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6xHYQYa3ePyLnNbDC3c5Ey/56d26d75e40a2d195e578a41b2b16612/panel_preferences.svg) , make sure **Stray Pages > Pad** is checked. If it isn't already, select it.
