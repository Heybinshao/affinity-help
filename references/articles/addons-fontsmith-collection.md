If you own a previous Affinity app—version 1 or 2 of Affinity Designer, Affinity Photo, or Affinity Publisher—you can claim the Fontsmith Collection, a curated, premium font library that early Affinity users can call their own.

Consisting of 450 font styles from 60 font families, the Fontsmith Collection for Affinity is a work of art in its own right. Expertly crafted by a team of talented type designers, every font is meticulously made, full of character, and ready to set the tone for whatever story you’re telling. There’s something for every type of text and every sort of project—and you can use the fonts for both personal and commercial work.

**For Affinity V2 users:**If you purchased Affinity V2 through a third-party app store (Mac App Store, iPad App Store, or Microsoft Store), you’ll be prompted to create or sign in to an Affinity ID after your initial in-app purchase. If you skipped that step, you can register later by selecting Account on the toolbar (desktop) or from the New Document dialog.

After signing in or creating an account, your fonts will automatically sync to your Affinity account for use in Affinity V2 and the new Affinity app. Just make sure you have an internet connection the next time you open either version.

To use these fonts in the new Affinity app, you’ll need to link your Affinity ID to your Canva account outlined above.

**For Affinity V1 users:**When you first open the app, a registration window appears. If you skipped it, you can reopen it by selecting My Account on the toolbar. Sign in with your existing Affinity ID or create a new one. This will register your purchase.

Please note that the fonts cannot be accessed in V1, so to use these fonts, you’ll need to download the new Affinity and link your Affinity ID to your Canva account.

This offer is only for customers who purchased Affinity before November 1, 2025.
