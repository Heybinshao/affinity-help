3.   Saving and loading selections

You can save pixel selections and reinstate them at a later time.

![Image 1: Pixel selection](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/TvsipZQ86GpG45KMuTl8P/72b5d35bcbf42c0763b41bf63529bd6b/save_load_selection.jpg)

Subject pixel selection indicated by the marching ants lines.

Any number of selections can be stored as spare alpha channels in the **Channels** panel for future use. From there, you can reinstate any selection on demand. To learn more see the dedicated Pixel selections from channels topic.

Instead of using the Channels panel, selections can be saved to a standalone file. Saved selections can then be loaded from the file into the same or another project.

*    On the **Channels** panel and with a pixel selection in place, **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) the Pixel Selection entry and select **Create Spare Channel**. 

The selection is stored at the bottom of the Channels panel as a new 'Spare Channel' entry.

On the **Channels** panel, **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) the Spare Channel entry which lets you:

*   **Rename**—change the Spare Channel's name.
*   **Delete**—delete the Spare Channel.
*   **Duplicate**—make a copy of the Spare Channel.
*   **Load to Pixel Selection**—the saved selection is added as a new selection on the page.
*   **Add To/Subtract From/Intersect With Pixel Selection**—the saved selection is added to, subtracted from, or intersected with an existing selection on the page.
*   **Load to "Layer name" channel**—loads the selection to a channel (e.g., Red) if the channel's layer was previously selected in the **Layers** panel.

The selection is stored at the bottom of the Channels panel as a new Spare Channel entry.

1.   With a pixel selection in place, from the **Pixel** menu, select **Pixel Selection > Save > To File**.
2.   Specify where to save the selection, as required.
3.   Click **Save**.

1.   On the **Pixel** menu, select **Pixel Selection > Load From File**.
2.   Navigate to the **.afselection** target file with the selection to load.
3.   Click **Open**.
