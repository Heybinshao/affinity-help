Canva AI's Select Subject feature uses advanced algorithms to precisely identify and isolate complex subjects in your photos, including those with detailed textures like hair, fur, or irregular edges. This intelligent selection method significantly reduces manual work, providing cleaner, more accurate selections faster than traditional methods. By analyzing pixel-level detail, the AI ensures consistent, high-quality results, simplifying your workflow even when working with challenging subjects.

Although this feature doesn't have any customizable options, you can adjust selections by adding to or subtracting from them using Affinity’s raster selection tools. Selections can also be refined, masked, or edited as you normally would with these tools.

Canva AI features run on secure servers, which means your data is processed online to generate images. However, your personal information stays private and anonymous at all times. Canva does not collect any user data—including hardware details, location, or workflow activity—as part of this process.
