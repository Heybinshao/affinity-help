# Aligning - Affinity Help Center

3.   Aligning

Objects and layer content can be aligned with each other using the Transform panel or to other page elements using alignment handles.

![Image 1: After](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/20LsosrZyisixuq1a5wn85/b3e2abad1cb5f64e9105b214e3b098ff/align_after.jpg)

After alignment is applied.

![Image 2: Before](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1B7qCJRzyumRQuVmiiGVwE/4637fb9e3359d2031a92dfee0f825b52/align_before.jpg)

Before alignment applied.

1.   Select multiple objects or layer content.
2.   Do one of the following: 
    *   In the Vector Studio ![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2Mejuekyv1U5fQVTYLqGud/edc8c0247142081ce0079de3013e815d/persona_vector.svg) , click **Alignment**![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2sBtuXOz6xHeHKSpdRiV0E/52b485eb1e9c69adb61dfee2adefd40f/arrange.svg)  on the Toolbar, then:
        *   Select an **Align Horizontally** or **Align Vertically** option. To distribute, select **Space Horizontally**![Image 5: Space Horizontally](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7Gf2oAR3MFpAZFPeawdVfN/dc366ff9436d6334d734bf6196634e13/space_horizontally.svg) or **Space Vertically**![Image 6: Space Vertically](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7dDWSQAguao9gyl0H9tl9k/8bc96b0f38764f674e2e629518b98a11/space_vertically.svg) .
        *   Click **Apply**.

    *   On the **Layer** menu, select an alignment option on the **Arrange > Align** submenu.

The context toolbar settings let you align in relation to selection bounds or to a _key object_. The latter by **⌥**-clicking (Mac) / **Alt**-clicking (Windows) on a designated object to align to (the key object is identified with a strong blue outline).

To align across different layers, **Edit within current container** needs to be enabled.

*   On the **Layers** panel, click **Edit within current container**![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7Mk5jz4pzwPhVhM6CPJAOg/b3d17c0013c768b2e5861e717791947e/edit_all_layers.svg) .

When enabled, alignment handles appear as you drag objects, allowing you to place them precisely by lining them up in accordance with other objects in your document. Alignment handles are especially useful for aligning the edges or centers of multiple selected objects to another page object or guide.

1.   With an object (or objects) selected, click on the **Move Tool**.
2.   On the context toolbar, select **Settings**![Image 8](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7e47FwYJ0eBW8yOWy3elSz/813ccca2419c99817334e5bb65c2a0db/cog_icon_2.svg) , and enable **Show Alignment Handles**. When selected, alignment handles are displayed at the center and edges of the selected object. Hovering over these handles displays a floating guideline across the page.
3.   Drag the selected object via any alignment handle (center or edge) to align it to a page element on the spread. This position can be snapped for accuracy.
