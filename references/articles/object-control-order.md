3.   Ordering

Every object or layer on the page occupies a specific position within a design's z-order (stacking order). When overlapping occurs, this will determine which parts of the design are exposed and which are hidden.

When objects or layers are placed or drawn, they are assigned a position in the document's z-order. This initial position is determined by targeting. However, all objects or layers can be repositioned within a layer's z-order as needed.

1.   Select one or more objects.
2.   Do one of the following: 
    *   In the Layout Studio ![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4BbduuJ3zEK1FT2Ohg1aLs/98f305a0ad579187aa758ae37dc956e6/layout_studio.svg)  or the Vector Studio ![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2Mejuekyv1U5fQVTYLqGud/edc8c0247142081ce0079de3013e815d/persona_vector.svg) , on the Toolbar, choose one of the following from the **Arrange**![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1QIM2hudO0XQiU5I3LmMS5/8f2509d1e2758def9bbba9302bbbf8ed/move_to_back.svg)  flyout: 
        *   ![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1QIM2hudO0XQiU5I3LmMS5/8f2509d1e2758def9bbba9302bbbf8ed/move_to_back.svg) **Send to Back**—repositions the selected object(s) at the bottom of the layer.
        *   ![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1fs2FjgUbNRpgly34SUjMa/1b6827df2b7885bc08d1410f138722b6/back_one.svg) **Back One**—moves the selected object(s) down one position in the layer.
        *   ![Image 8](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3pmUOWuYG9wEM0ObnF9MHv/37a012753b8d30074da0433a454cb54c/forward_one.svg) **Forward One**—moves the selected object(s) up one position in the layer.
        *   ![Image 9](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1oANgUVUQRW0i0Lo7QDply/d2badae8e5f5dbd7306f3c8e7f1da8a9/move_to_front.svg) **Move to Front**—repositions the selected object(s) at the top of the layer.

    *   On the **Layer** menu, select an option on the **Arrange > Move** submenu.
    *   On the **Layers** panel, drag a layer entry up/down the layer stack. When you see a blue line between two layers, drop the layer to place.
