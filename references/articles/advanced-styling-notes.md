# Styling notes - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/advanced-styling-notes/

The Notes panel allows you to control various attributes of how footnotes, sidenotes and endnotes are presented.

At the top of the **Notes** panel, specify how extensively the panel's settings will affect notes of the selected type:

*   **Document-wide**—settings affect all notes of the selected type in your document.
*   **Custom**—settings affect only notes encompassed by your selection or at the insertion point. They are also applied when you insert a note.

The Custom option lets some notes use different settings than the document defaults.

For example, you might use a different number format for a specific story, or apply a different rule color, character style, or paragraph style that does not clash with a specific page's background.

The scope of changes depends on the text selection or insertion point.

*   When a text frame is selected, all references in the frame and their note bodies are affected. (Notes in unselected linked frames are not affected.)
*   When all of a story's text is selected, all the story's references and their note bodies are affected. (Notes in other stories are not affected.)
*   When some of a story's text is selected, only the references in the selection and their note bodies are affected.
*   When the insertion point is at a reference in story text, only that reference and its note body are affected.
*   When the selection or insertion point is within a note body, the note body and its reference (in story text) are affected.

The sections on the Notes panel change depending on the selected note type on the panel. Some sections are available only when you select certain secondary settings on the panel, as described below.

**Available for:** Footnotes, sidenotes and endnotes

**Purpose:** Note serialization settings, including characters used for references.

**Available for:** Footnotes, sidenotes and endnotes

**Purpose:** Advanced reference formatting, and character and paragraph styles for references and note bodies.

**Available for:** Footnotes, sidenotes, and endnotes at End of Story

**Purpose:** Distances of note bodies from story text and each other.

**Available for:** Footnotes, and endnotes at **End of Story**

**Purpose:** Style settings for lines that separate note bodies from story text.

**Available for:** Endnotes in **Separate Frame**, **Shared Section Frame**, **Shared Document Frame** or **End of Book**

**Purpose:**Text and paragraph style for the heading that precedes endnote bodies.

The Notes panel's **Rules** section allows you to clearly distinguish footnote bodies from the preceding story text by drawing a separating line between them.

Use **Rule before** to choose which scenario to style:

*   **First Note**—rules drawn above groups of note bodies where the first line is a new note.
*   **Continued Note**—rules drawn above groups of note bodies where the first line is a continuation of a note body from an earlier page. (A note's reference is not repeated where it continues.)

For each scenario, you can specify whether a rule should be drawn at all, and how it should be styled, sized, and positioned.

When endnote bodies are in a separate text frame after their references, section, document or book, each is enclosed in zero-width, non-printing note marks (brackets).
