# Macro panel - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/panels-macro-panel/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Design fundamentals](https://www.affinity.studio/help/design-fundamentals/)
3.   Macro panel

The Macro panel provides a means of recording, saving, importing and exporting macros—a series of actions and operations that can be quickly reproduced and applied to speed up and aid workflows.

For Mac/Windows: On the **Window** menu, select **Pixel > Macro**.

For more information on how to record, edit and use macros, see [Macros](https://www.affinity.studio/help/macros-batch-macros/).

The following options are available on the panel:

*   ![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4eZ2LqylY1lDAuxfr1HpJi/26d0b3adaa1493e8020c1abce8a54ccf/ImportMacro.svg) [**Import**](https://www.affinity.studio/help/addons-importing-addons/)—imports a previously exported .afmacro file. 
*   ![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5CVvlRmozd7kkxSpn7z3D9/194b5cd0ab3e4691935ad70040fa32c0/ExportMacro.svg) **Export**—exports your recorded macro to a .afmacro file which can be shared with other Affinity users or stored externally from the app.
*   ![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7p1Dg588fVYcfCEarOJ7o7/6402dfeeb62608da34ec6c5af6129f12/AddMacroToLibrary.svg) **Add to Library**—saves your recorded macro to the macro library. A macro needs to be saved or it will be lost on subsequent macro recording.
*   ![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3AFNVriMaPCBIxtmGAPzDE/ce950e27ab66766ee7cae04c8286691d/reset_macro.svg) **Reset**—click to clear the currently recorded macro operations and actions. Use to start recording from scratch again.
*   ![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1cwdJYIzfPu5VTNjjDN7hL/fd4a2523a8279d494854eb71ba15848a/record_macro.png) **Start recording**—click to record your actions and operations which are added to the panel as you record.
*   ![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/22USxQXzqAUqCt8dMbf6Cw/f6c5c9beea315ba9f9971aca9c3088f0/stop_recording_macro.png) **Stop recording**—click to stop recording your macro.
*   ![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3eqtj1FgaIwedY6Nz8loNJ/b2e93f0292fd54ed4751952d94df5bfb/playback_macro.png) **Play**—click to play back and preview your recorded macro.
*   ![Image 8](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7e47FwYJ0eBW8yOWy3elSz/813ccca2419c99817334e5bb65c2a0db/cog_icon_2.svg) **Edit**—click the cog icon to customize a specific operation's settings for the already recorded macro.
*   ![Image 9](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4XvGqFQtu9zgML3ybuNZfG/95c5b03a460bd37768a670311b6eb5f6/checkbox_tick.svg) **Disable/Enable macro operation**—uncheck to exclude the operation from playback; check to include it again.

![Image 10](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6xHYQYa3ePyLnNbDC3c5Ey/56d26d75e40a2d195e578a41b2b16612/panel_preferences.svg)

 The following options are available on the **Panel Preferences** menu:

*   **Panels**—opens a dialog where you can quickly set the visibility of all panels in the current Studio.
*   **Close**—hides the current panel.
*   **Close Panel Group**—hides the current panel and any others grouped with it.
