# Optical alignment - Affinity Help Center

3.   Optical alignment

Optical alignment uses preset or custom rules for aligning characters at the start and end of paragraph text, as well as at tab stops.

![Image 1: After](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4gESUZBsIELdjfSBW3xRvq/7e9634893fe7d85d2cc6cbf8b4355afd/opticalalign_after.jpg)

![Image 2: Before](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4xPZI5DqcXVqUiYNyjfvvN/7ed16c1c4adec9cf86f0b8853d2c9138/opticalalign_before.jpg)

Optical alignment is often used for hanging punctuation—where marks like commas or quotes stick out slightly from the beginning or end of lines of text.

It's best to apply optical alignment to a base paragraph style (e.g., _Base_). You can fine-tune it later on the Character panel if needed. There's no need to manually select a character to adjust its alignment. The rules apply _automatically_ when specific characters, such as quotation marks, occur at the beginning or end of a line, or at a tab stop.

There are two types of optical alignment: _Manual_, which uses a lookup table of rules, and _Font_, which relies on built-in support in the font itself.

1.   On the **Text Styles** panel, on the required style's Options menu ![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2XLuXTwmEh714qaYONrPKP/6d26123960257b3233a3eb084188eda7/moremenuicon.svg)  and select **Edit "<style name>"**.
2.   On the dialog that appears: 
    1.   In the **Character** section, select **Optical Alignment**.
    2.   Set **Type** to _Manual_.
    3.   (Optional) Add custom optical alignment rules for problematic characters in the style's font.
    4.   Click **OK**.

When you select manual optical alignment, Affinity automatically adds rule presets for characters that commonly require optical alignment. They can be edited or deleted.

The presets and any custom optical alignment rules you add will affect text to which the style is applied.

Accurate optical alignment depends on the font used, so it's worth inspecting the alignment if you apply a different font.

1.   Follow the procedure for applying optical alignment, but keep the dialog open.
2.   Click **Add**. A new rule entry appears first in the list of rules.
3.   Edit the rule as follows: 
    *   **Left**—the percentage of the character's width to be outdented at paragraph's Left Indent or tab stop; 100% means the whole character will be outdented.
    *   **Right**—the percentage of the character's width to be outdented at paragraph's Right Indent or tab stop.
    *   **Characters**—the characters to be optically aligned. When one of the characters occurs at the start or end of a line, the optical alignment rule will be applied to it.

4.   Click **OK**.

Optical alignment rules can also be applied as local formatting, on the **Character** panel's **Optical Alignment** section.
