3.   Margins

The margin is the area between the main content of the page and the page edges (or spread and spread edges). Margins are indicated by a blue solid line, but this color can be changed.

![Image 1: Margins](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3Hdkn2PgVtL9EOg7WuyVtK/d30aeec662c2ad980a63dac7862670c6/margins.png)

Default margin (A) in relation to page edge (B).

![Image 2: Margins](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7Jp0dlfXABFNESDmfzh8MM/116fca71db7525debd292e25ef00e734/margins_publisher.png)

For page layout, uniform margins on single page spread (A) and mirrored margins on facing page spread (B).

Page margins can be set up with each new document at document setup, and can then be edited at a later point.

Page margins give an indication of the extent of the page that will be printed when using a desktop printer. Design elements positioned outside the margins may not be printed. Margins can be set evenly or unevenly depending on the page orientation and can be customized manually or using the settings of an installed printer. They are non-printing and non-exporting.

When determining the size of your page margin, it is important to consider how text-intensive the design will be, as well as the page layout and orientation. For printing purposes, you may for example consider allotting additional space to the inside margins of facing pages so text does not disappear in the page gutter.

For page layouts, margins can be inherited from any assigned master page, replacing the page's current margin settings.

Design elements positioned outside the margins may not be printed.

Margins can also be used for snapping to when creating or moving objects or layer content.

If you intend on exporting your design, rather than printing it directly, you can design with complete freedom from margins. If this is the case, you may wish to switch off the margins.

1.   On the **File** menu, select **New**.
2.   On the dialog, check **Include margins**.
3.    Do one of the following: 
    *   Set the **Left**, **Right**, **Top**, and **Bottom** margins.
    *   For _facing-page spreads_: Set the **Inner**, **Outer**, **Top**, and **Bottom** margins. If you have **Default master** checked, the margin settings will be the property of the master page.

*   On the **View** menu, select **Margins and Guides**.
*   On the dialog that appears, edit the values in the **Margins** section.

1.   (Optional) On the **Pages** panel, select page thumbnails to include/exclude in the margin adjustment.
2.   On the **Pages** panel, **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) any selected page and choose **Spread Properties**.
3.   Enable **Whole Document**, **Selected Masters** or **Selected Spreads** as appropriate.
4.   Change margin values in the **Margins** section.

*   On the **View** menu, deselect/select **Show > Margins**. A check mark is displayed next to the menu item when the margins are visible.

*   On the **View** menu, select **Margins and Guides**. 
*   On the dialog that appears, in the **Margins** section, click the **Color** swatch and select a new margin color.
