3.   Mixing paint colors

Affinity gives you the opportunity to digitally simulate the mixing of wet paint applied to a canvas.

![Image 1: Mixing colors example](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/0vuuwH77OfCwkJcvFTmiz/4979c0427c5f3fbabf6b8843f4d05ec1/paintmixer.png)

Effect of dragging the Paint Mixer Brush across color boundaries.

The **Paint Mixer Brush** is pre-loaded with the Foreground color selected on the Color panel. As you drag on the page, this color blends with the color of pixels underneath the stroke, giving a smudged appearance to the result.

By default, the mixed color remains on the Paint Mixer Brush. However, the brush can be manually 'cleaned' at any time. As soon as you drag on the page with a clean brush, the brush will pick up the color directly underneath it.

Alternatively, the mixed color can be manually removed from the brush at any time and reloaded with the Foreground color. You can also set this to occur automatically every time you begin a new stroke.

The **Paint Mixer Brush** only responds to the **Size** and **Rotation** options.
