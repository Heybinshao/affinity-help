3.   Editing channels

You can select one or more channels for further editing. The selected channel is indicated on the panel by both the Visible as well as the Editable icons.

![Image 1: Separated out color channels of a RGB image.](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/spWxm8NR65BXJNWoEXUWz/5f32d8306c76d1e08b473df2ae808d87/editing-channels.jpg)

(A) Composite Green channel selected and editable. (B) Spare Channel created.

The Channels panel displays a list of channels based on current document information. Pixel Selection will always appear at the bottom of the panel even when no selection is active.

Do one of the following:

*   On the **Channels** panel, click **Visible**![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7eblP76vdFdrp2uaZ3g2uC/4609ca56c51ddfe4b1aeb480e5ac5d9e/view.svg)  on the channel entry.
*    On the **Channels** panel, **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) the channel you wish to target and select **Toggle Visible** from the pop-out menu. 

Do one of the following:

*   On the **Channels** panel, click **Editable**![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/52wwmEt1j8O6TCIaPBrjd9/477c573cc18e65028a3fe8de9f965bfa/channel_editable.svg)  on the channel entry. A grayed out icon means the channel is no longer editable.
*    On the **Channels** panel, **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) the channel you wish to target and select **Toggle Editable** from the pop-out menu. 

The spare channel that stores your mask can be isolated for editing. This lets you paint and erase directly on the spare channel as well as apply filters such as Gaussian Blur for softer mask edges.

1.    On the **Channels** panel, **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) each of the **Background** channels in turn and select **Create Spare Channel**. 
2.   (Optional) Rename the newly created spare channels.
3.   **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) the spare channel entry to isolate it and select **Load to Pixel Selection**. 
4.    (Optional) Select another spare channel and **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) and select **Subtract From Pixel Selection**. This helps if there is an undesired overspill of color onto areas. 
5.   Add an Adjustment of choice.
6.   On the **Pixel** menu, select **Pixel Selection > Deselect**.
7.   Return to the Adjustment and modify it further, as required.

Before editing a spare channel, you have the option of duplicating the initial spare channel. This procedure is often used to preserve an unedited backup copy of a spare channel before editing the original.

*   **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) the spare channel entry and select the option. 

The duplicate is stored below the original as a new spare channel entry.

1.   **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) the spare channel entry and select **Load To Pixel Selection**. 
2.   Click the Pixel Selection's thumbnail.

The **Quick Mask** option on the Toolbar will be enabled.
