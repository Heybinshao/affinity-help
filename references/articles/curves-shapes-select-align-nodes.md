3.   Selecting and aligning nodes

When editing curves or shapes you can select multiple nodes in different ways. With selection control, you can then align and distribute nodes as you would with multiple selected objects, as well as transform selected nodes.

![Image 1: node select](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6dFvNpdcQWX4qwUru2BOEO/3d759d75fb6e1bac64c322262ea6a897/node_Select.jpg)

Selecting node-by-node, by marquee and by lasso.

![Image 2: node align distribute](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5BiQAI5MT0L8d3fAonIAdu/c3164f9bc7818d63a489d7ec2c39ef35/node_SelectAlignDistribute.jpg)

Aligning and distributing selected nodes.

This feature applies to vector content (not raster content), and specifically to curves or closed shapes drawn with the Pen Tool; the nodes of text objects or geometric shapes can also be manipulated but only after they are converted to curves.

Use the [ and ] keys to jump forwards and backwards through nodes on a curve.

*    With the **Node Tool**![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2Tyrn620F9iCYiF39DxVOc/9de008f998af69eafee088eb5a3c5210/node_tool.svg)  and curve/shape selected, do one of the following: 
    *   Node-by-node selection: With the **⇧** key (Mac) / **Shift** key (Windows) pressed, click individual nodes in turn.
    *   Marquee selection: Drag a marquee selection area over nodes to include them in your selection.
    *   Lasso selection: With the **⌥** key (Mac) / **Alt** key (Windows) pressed, either: 
        *   Draw freehand around nodes to encompass them in the blue selection area.
        *   Draw a polygonal area around nodes taking a click-by-click approach.

To deselect a node from a multiple selection, click it while the **⇧** key (Mac) / **Shift** key (Windows) is pressed.

1.   With the **Node Tool** and curve/shape selected, select one or more nodes on the curve or shape as above.
2.   Do one of the following: 
    *   On the Toolbar, click **Alignment**![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2sBtuXOz6xHeHKSpdRiV0E/52b485eb1e9c69adb61dfee2adefd40f/arrange.svg) , set the **Align To** option, then an **Align Horizontally** or **Align Vertically** option from the pop-up panel. To distribute, choose a **Space Horizontally** or **Space Vertically** option from the end of the icon row. Click **Apply**.
    *   On the **Layer** menu, select an alignment option on the **Arrange > Align** submenu.
