## Shooting tips

Here are a few things to consider when shooting.

*   To help image alignment, use a tripod or ensure your camera is in a fixed position.
*   Set white balance manually to avoid large differences in tone between the shots.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/stacking-stacks-creative/#how-to-create-a-stack)

### How to create a stack

Desktop app

1.   On the **File** menu, select **New Image Process > Stack**.
2.   From the dialog, click **Add** to locate and select your images, and then **Open** to add the images to the stack list.
3.   Click **OK**.
4.   On the **Layers** panel, experiment with the **Stack Mode**. **Median**  ![Image 12](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2dBbxjKizPoQwjjJnwuk7I/943a885370a07ac33fd61784efd6a532/median.png)        is selected by default, but try **Maximum** and **Range** in particular for more creative effects.

**Median** stack mode is selected by default, but try **Outlier** and **Range** in particular for a sharper and creative effect.

Experiment with **Stack Mode** settings to achieve a variety of creative effects.
