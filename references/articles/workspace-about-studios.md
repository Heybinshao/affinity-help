# About Studios - Affinity Help Center

3.   About Studios

A Studio is a workspace that groups tools and panels specific to a design discipline (e.g., vector design, photo editing or page layout) or tailored to a custom way of working. They show in Affinity as Toolbar icons that can be swapped between.

![Image 1: Affinity's core design Studios](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2xVVPpElu88idrZJzuFS1i/e168dab63cf70fdf62f1333c20743212/studio_switcher.png)

Vector, Pixel and Layout Studios swapping

Here's an overview of the types of Studio available to you:

*   ![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2Mejuekyv1U5fQVTYLqGud/edc8c0247142081ce0079de3013e815d/persona_vector.svg) **Vector Studio**—this Studio hosts your core vector-design tools. It lets you create illustrations, digital/print projects, logos, icons, UI designs and device mock-ups.
*   ![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/x0qUKV0QmljBpXjlKaAyD/48b1503c7d6cc8f05d91c6af70f31b85/persona_photo.svg) **Pixel Studio**—this Studio hosts your core photo-editing tools including painting and retouch tools. It's also perfect for digital art including beautiful raster paintings, complex raster patterns and so much more. You can also add raster textures to vector designs created in the Vector or Layout Studios.
*   ![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4BbduuJ3zEK1FT2Ohg1aLs/98f305a0ad579187aa758ae37dc956e6/layout_studio.svg) **Layout Studio**—for publishing-based page layout features and tools. The Studio excels at professional page layout, bringing text and placed imagery together for digital or pro-print delivery of books, brochures, magazines, posters and more.
*   ![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/27LBjHSuPkk8IgU8EJlRTG/2b922a6b2594fb1d65d51af81c0daeaa/canva_ai_studio.svg) **Canva AI Studio**—for a mix of powerful AI-generated Canva tools and machine learning tools based on intelligent generative technologies. Great for a vast array of image manipulation needs.
*   ![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3vfODAvo895dTN5bFMTLNs/68e3d14904308a323dbf48d056fcf21d/persona_slice.svg) **Slice Studio*******—for exporting artboards, layers, groups, objects or regions of your image as export slices to different file formats and image sizes simultaneously.
*   ![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/Ccz93mQlfYjSj56IXciai/24943c22022c4add0f6c21a083fe00db/retouching_studio.svg) **Retouching Studio*******—the Studio brings together tools and panels suited to retouching images.
*   ![Image 8](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3hx1hj8hGx6Hpnxk4ENsNr/05f96b573a44a9955c1e2f8a67db4b20/colourgrading_studio.svg) **Color Grading Studio*******—this Studio is suitable for changing the style and look of your image by affecting tone and color.
*   ![Image 9: Typography Studio](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/34xD29QwOxAhfW1bEoikli/ebd4ec8d9439b693d3b90d17cd58e8fe/typography_studio.svg) **Typography Studio***—the Studio presents tools and panels that are focused on text formatting and styling.
*   ![Image 10](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3HLsf5xx2FlnBTmx7VwAkX/eb93eb77aa0dc236a5edfe2d6e7ef9f7/compositing_studio.svg) **Compositing Studio***—use tools and panels that bring multiple images and assets together creatively.

***** Not displayed by default. Additional Studios are available via context toolbars or menus:

*   **Liquify Studio**—for distortion of image areas. Great for retouch warping and special warping effects.
*   **Develop Studio**—processes your RAW images with full control of image color, tone and lens correction.
*   **Tone Mapping Studio**—applies tone mapping to your HDR and non-HDR imagery, bringing out global and local contrast.

Any Studio can be customized. You'll be able to:

*   switch on/off tools in the Studio.
*   move tools between Studios.
*   change the Toolbar arrangement and switch on/off its options.
*   change the context toolbar position.
*   change panel visibility, grouping and positioning.

See [Customizing Studios](https://www.affinity.studio/help/workspace-customizing-studios/).

By switching Studios to access your chosen discipline, only tools and panels relevant to the current discipline are shown so the interface remains uncluttered, thus improving your workflow and general ease of use. This ensures maximum efficiency and design focus.

Do one of the following:

*   On the Toolbar, click the button for the Studio that you want to use.
*   For Mac: On the **Affinity** menu, click **Studios** and select from the pop-up menu.
*   For Windows: On the **File** menu, click **Studios** and select from the pop-up menu.

Liquify, Develop or Tone Mapping Studios are accessed via **Pixel > Filters**.
