# Navigating pages - Affinity Help Center

2.   [Page layout](https://www.affinity.studio/help/page-layout/)
3.   Navigating pages

You can navigate a document's pages using various methods to locate the one you want.

You can use the **Pages** panel to find a page by its thumbnail, or scroll the document view.

Alternatively, use the **page navigation bar** at the bottom left of the workspace to advance through your document one page/spread at a time, or to jump to the first or last page or a specific page number.

![Image 1: Page navigation bar](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6tp7k9NyZgO3NrcjA8dm87/7b1396ffd8bbee986be2f772ead395d5/pagenavigationbar.png)

The page navigation bar

Do one of the following:

*   On the **Page navigation bar**, click one of the following: 
    *   **First Spread**![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/uQL0aobmTlfmPc49O21Sh/0d4d0fe391454acb3c74bfdd46eff77e/first_spread.svg) 
    *   **Previous Spread**![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5MKcLv1phncFOVph8IzYK3/1de85c43ce50bcd6656e956ad12832b8/previous_spread.svg) 
    *   **Next Spread**![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5Wpr6NGmLTO7Eops8nKscO/36bed17b1c84f01a0d27646355d19d31/next_spread.svg) 
    *   **Last Spread**![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/666YGPvHMHzlebY6iSmk7r/7d3ba84642a7b30137075b4c74bfa573/last_spread.svg) .

*   On the **Page navigation bar**, click in the page number box, type a page number, then press the **⏎** key (Mac) / **Return** key (Windows).
*   On the **Pages** panel, double-click the thumbnail of the page (or master page) you want to view.

Commands corresponding to the **Page navigation bar**'s functionality are available on the **Document** menu, and can be assigned keyboard shortcuts in Affinity's Settings.

*   On the **Page navigation bar**, click **Toggle Master View**![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5m51TkXv9agm7yqcBSjKw8/3c2cfa8d4d1c44056b989f38e5cbe9fe/switch_to_master.svg) .

*   On the **Page navigation bar**, click **Previously Viewed Page**![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/Hfb67prrkNss0nLROdr8e/b2429f2ec1fac550901b9ced6388a6d6/previously_viewed_spread.svg) .

The previously viewed page is the one whose page number(s) or name was last shown on the **Page navigation bar**. It may be a publication page/spread or a master page.

Affinity remembers the previously viewed page's position and zoom level in the document view.

Repeated selection of this command allows you to instantly navigate between two parts of a publication.
