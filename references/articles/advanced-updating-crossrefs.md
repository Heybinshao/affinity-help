3.   Updating cross-references

If a document changes, you may need to tell Affinity to update your cross-references.

An **out-of-date** or **invalid** cross-reference will be flagged by Preflight. This can happen after you:

*   add or delete pages.
*   change a section's name.
*   amend publication page numbering.
*   edit paragraph text at a cross-reference's target.
*   remove an anchor that is a cross-reference's target.
*   (Desktop only) use a _Chapter Name_ subfield in a document whose parent book is not open on the **Books** panel.

This feature is only available in Affinity for desktop.

If your document contains cross-references to other book chapters, make sure the book is open on the **Books** panel before updating.

The Books panel includes options for updating cross-references: either immediately in all chapters, or automatically when exporting or printing.

On the **Cross-References** panel:

1.   Select one or more cross-references.
2.   Click **Update Cross-Reference**![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6XPtcMeQlUh8KquozxyStX/4a3c0b99a5017eb42ef0d629268ccf53/update.svg) .

Alternatively, make an insertion point at a cross-reference. Then, on the **Text** menu, select **Cross-References > Update**.

On the **Cross-References** panel:

*   Click **Update All Cross-References**![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1pMorKRcRu6WurDelronke/05f8333aca67d2295ad1e7d770a6332e/update_all.svg) .

Alternatively, on the **Text** menu, select **Cross-References > Update All**.

On the **Books** panel's **Panel Preferences** menu

![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6xHYQYa3ePyLnNbDC3c5Ey/56d26d75e40a2d195e578a41b2b16612/panel_preferences.svg)

:

*   Click **Cross-References > Update Cross-References**.

When exporting or printing a book, Affinity can update cross-references automatically. To enable this, turn on **Cross-References > Update Cross-References Before Output** (on the **Books** panel's **Panel Preferences** menu).
