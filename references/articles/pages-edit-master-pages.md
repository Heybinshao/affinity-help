# Editing master pages

You can edit a master page just like any other page in your publication.

![Image 6: Example master and publication pages](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/OnJidRjQuj8IjKGA0xMCP/060fa6f6cafebec38b7ee0c60e9b4be9/masterpageedit.jpg)

Master page (top) with introduced placeholder picture frame (red) showing automatically on publication page (bottom).

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/pages-edit-master-pages/#editing-multi-page-masters)

## Editing multi-page masters

You can't change how many pages a master has or its spine position. Make a new master page for each version you need.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/pages-edit-master-pages/#how-to-edit-a-master-page-s-content)

### How to edit a master page's content

Desktop app

1.   On the **Pages** panel, double-click the master page's thumbnail.
2.   Add or change content on the master page like you do on a publication page.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/pages-edit-master-pages/#how-to-edit-a-master-page-s-properties)

### How to edit a master page's properties

Desktop app

1.   On the **Pages** panel, **^(ctrl)**-click (Mac) / **right**-click (Windows) the master page, then select **Spread Properties**.
2.   On the dialog that appears: 
    1.   (Optional) To edit some pages, choose **Selected Pages**, then select the pages in the list.
    2.   Change the master's name, size, scaling settings, or margin settings as needed.
    3.   Select **OK**.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/pages-edit-master-pages/#how-to-rename-a-master-page)

### How to rename a master page

Desktop app

On the **Pages** panel:

1.   Click the master page's thumbnail.
2.   Click the master page's name, type a new name, then press the **⏎** key (Mac) / **Return** key (Windows).

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/pages-edit-master-pages/#how-to-duplicate-master-pages)

### How to duplicate master pages

Desktop app

1.   On the **Pages** panel, click the master page's thumbnail.
2.   Click **Duplicate**  ![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/RJpVOUJCTbGYUesRprC7s/cab464a26fbad0fa2eb00f3e300ecf32/duplicate_master_pages.svg)       . The duplicate page is added immediately after the original page.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/pages-edit-master-pages/#how-to-remove-a-master-page)

### How to remove a master page

Desktop app

1.   On the **Pages** panel, click the master page's thumbnail.
2.   Click **Delete Selected Master**  ![Image 8](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3m8ciDtLqf07Yrma1i0j4x/202c1d508104bbb0a1290c5be7725eae/trash_can.svg)       .
