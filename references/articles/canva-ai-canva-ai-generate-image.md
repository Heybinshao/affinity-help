## About Generate Image

Canva AI's Generate Image feature turns your creative ideas—for digital art, vector graphics, or high quality photos—into life. Just describe the output you want, and Canva AI will generate multiple versions for you to choose from. This is especially useful when gathering assets for complex or layered designs.

Canva AI features run on secure servers, which means your data is processed online to generate images. However, your personal information stays private and anonymous at all times. Canva does not collect any user data—including hardware details, location, or workflow activity—as part of this process.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-generate-image/#choosing-an-ai-model)

## **Choosing an AI model**

When using **Generate Image/Vector**, you can select between different AI models. These determine how images are generated, including output quality, processing speed, and behaviour when credits are used.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-generate-image/#available-models)

### **Available models**

**Premium models**

*   **Leonardo Lucid Origin** — Provides balanced, general-purpose image generation suitable for a wide range of prompts and styles.

Premium models provide standard generation capabilities and are suitable for general use.

When using Premium models:

*   You can continue generating images after your available credits are used.
*   A short pause is applied between generations once credits are depleted.

This makes Premium models suitable for iterative workflows where continued access is required, even with reduced generation speed.

**Ultra models**

*   **Nano Banana** — Fast, general-purpose model for generating consistent results across varied subjects.
*   **SeeDream 4.5** — Produces more refined and stylised outputs with improved detail and visual consistency.
*   **Nano Banana 2 (2K)** — Generates higher-resolution results with enhanced clarity while maintaining fast performance.
*   **SeeDream 4.5 (UHD)** — Delivers the highest-quality output with maximum detail, supporting UHD image generation.

Ultra models provide enhanced generation capabilities and higher-quality output.

When using Ultra models:

*   Higher-resolution output is supported, including UHD generation.

Ultra models are best suited to workflows where image quality and uninterrupted generation are required.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-generate-image/#best-practice)

### **Best practice**

Select a model based on your workflow:

*   Use **Premium** for ongoing experimentation and concept development.
*   Use **Ultra** when producing final assets or higher-resolution outputs.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-generate-image/#how-to-use-generate-image:)

### How to use Generate Image:

Desktop app

1.   On the **Canva AI Studio**  ![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/27LBjHSuPkk8IgU8EJlRTG/2b922a6b2594fb1d65d51af81c0daeaa/canva_ai_studio.svg)       , navigate to the **Generate Image** tab.
2.   Select either **Image** or **Vector** output, as required.
3.   In the **Prompt** field, type a description of the image you'd like to create.
4.   (Optional) From the **More menu**, set the required **Style**, **Image count,** or **Aspect** for the output.
5.   (Optional) Add a reference image.
6.   Click **Generate**.

The generated image appears as a new pixel layer in the Layers panel, named after your prompt.

After generating multiple images, use the **Layers** panel to locate your outputs and continue with your edits, as needed.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-ai-canva-ai-generate-image/#how-to-select-and-ai-model:)

### How to select and AI model:

Desktop app

1.   On the **Generate Image** tab, locate the **AI Quality** option.
2.   Select either a **Premium** or **Ultra** model.
3.   (Optional) Adjust additional options such as style, aspect ratio, or image count.
4.   Click **Generate**.
