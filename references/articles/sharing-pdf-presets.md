# Compare PDF export presets - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/sharing-pdf-presets/

Affinity includes several presets for exporting to PDF, tailored to different uses.

The tables below compare the various presets' settings. Descriptions of each setting are available in the [Export Settings](https://www.affinity.studio/help/sharing-export-settings/) topic.

After selecting a preset, settings can be customized to suit your needs. For example, you may wish to lower JPEG quality to reduce your PDF's file size.
