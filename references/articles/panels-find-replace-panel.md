# Find and Replace panel - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/panels-find-replace-panel/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Design fundamentals](https://www.affinity.studio/help/design-fundamentals/)
3.   Find and Replace panel

Use the Find and Replace panel to search for and replace words, fonts, formats, and character attributes—globally or one at a time.

For Mac/Windows: On the **Window** menu, select **Text > Find and Replace**.

The following options are available on the panel:

*   **Find**—specify the item you would like to search for. 
    *   Select the magnifying glass icon to choose special characters to include in the search. The most recently used search terms are also listed here, and can be cleared by selecting **Clear Recent Finds**.
    *   ![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5GKMygAHyredYA3sp9qhwr/e6daff47a6c427bfb5491c9043a8be72/clear_x.png)  Select the cross to instantly clear the text from the box.

*   **Replace with**—type the replacement (if needed) for the item you are searching for. 
    *   Select the downward-pointing arrow to select special characters to include in the replacement term. The most recently used replacement terms are also listed here, and can be cleared by selecting **Clear Recent Replaces**.
    *   ![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5GKMygAHyredYA3sp9qhwr/e6daff47a6c427bfb5491c9043a8be72/clear_x.png)  Select the cross to instantly clear the text from the box.

*   ![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/7e47FwYJ0eBW8yOWy3elSz/813ccca2419c99817334e5bb65c2a0db/cog_icon_2.svg) **Formatting**—lets you precisely control the find and replace operation using the following options: 
    *   **Format**—select to open a dialog where you can choose the formatting you wish to find in the selected scope.
    *   **Character Style**—select the character style you wish to find in the selected scope.
    *   **Paragraph Style**—select the paragraph style you wish to find in the selected scope.
    *   **Reset Format**—select to clear any formatting settings you have selected.
    *   **Match Case**—select to only display search results matching the case of what you've specified in the **Find** box.
    *   **Match Whole Word Only**—select this option to exclude results that do not match the whole word from the search.
    *   **Expression type** (Find only)—tells Affinity whether what you've typed is: 
        *   literal text (**Normal**) you want to find.
        *   a pattern text must match (**Regular Expression** or **Locale Aware Regular Expression**).

    *   **Regular Expression Options** (Find only)—tells Affinity whether: 
        *   to match text across one line or several (**Single Line Mode**).
        *   a dot in your regular expression should match any character including or excluding paragraph breaks (**Dot Matches Paragraph Break**.

*   **Scope**—select how extensively Affinity will search for the specified item: _Document_, _Section_, _Spread_, _Page_, _Story_ or _Selection_. A selection can be a text range or one or more text objects.
*   **Find and Replace actions**—use your search options to either: 
    *   **Find**—searches for matches in your document and cycles through results.
    *   **Replace**—replaces the selected match with your specified content.
    *   **Replace All**—replaces all matches with your specified content.

*   **Results**—lists instances in your document that match your search options. Click an item to focus the document view on the matching content and select it.

![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6xHYQYa3ePyLnNbDC3c5Ey/56d26d75e40a2d195e578a41b2b16612/panel_preferences.svg)

 The following options are available on the **Panel Preferences** menu:

*   **Panels**—opens a dialog where you can quickly set the visibility of all panels in the current Studio.
*   **Close**—hides the current panel.
*   **Close Panel Group**—hides the current panel and any others grouped with it.
