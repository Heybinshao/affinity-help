2.   Selective Color adjustment

The Selective Color adjustment

![Image 1](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5pCdcS7eRLCyBYa1OyHpGX/a663142cc3e56cf060fbdd91be42d79c/selective_colour_adjustment_type.svg)

 provides a way to subtly adjust and enhance colors on an individual channel basis.

![Image 2: After](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/1tEGq818DT1GM9ANsyAdPs/f89ae1d63c526a3bbdcf86a188e7f6fb/adjustment_selectiveClr_after.jpg)

![Image 3: Black & White before](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5NXgmzFzXKve50c596UktV/3587c26a6e00f462f4d50c450a098ea1/adjustment_base.jpg)

This adjustment produces subtle effects. Even at the extreme ends of the adjustment, you shouldn't see many artefacts or saturated pixels. If you intend to make extreme adjustments, such as completely recoloring pixels, use the HSL adjustment instead.

*   **Color**—determines the color to be adjusted. Select from the pop-up menu.
*   **Relative**—when selected (default), color is added or subtracted in proportion to the amount of that color present in the source pixels, giving a more natural effect. If this option is off, colors are added or subtracted based on the absolute percentage specified, regardless of how much of that color is present in the image.
*   The sliders control the levels of the selected **Color**. Drag the slider to the left to decrease the level of the named color, drag the slider to the right to increase it.

*   On the **Layers** panel, click **Adjustments**![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4ZFx9I9M7MiUBPmytBqZrt/cad5a29a5e39b1b3faa42c5fa29402f3/add_adjustment_layer.svg)  and choose **Selective Color**.
*   On the **Adjustment** panel, select **Selective Color**![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5pCdcS7eRLCyBYa1OyHpGX/a663142cc3e56cf060fbdd91be42d79c/selective_colour_adjustment_type.svg)  and then click on a preset to modify, as required.
*   On the **Pixel** menu, select **New Adjustment Layer > Selective Color**.

The **Adjustment** panel is not visible by default. To enable it, navigate to the top menu and select **Window > Pixel > Adjustment**.
