# Canva for Nonprofits - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/canva-for-nonprofits/

1.   [Help Center](https://www.affinity.studio/help/)
2.   [Account and billing](https://www.affinity.studio/help/account-billing/)
3.   Canva for Nonprofits

If you're on a mission to create greater impact, Canva can help.

If your organization is an eligible nonprofit, you can access Canva Pro for free as part of our [Canva for Nonprofits](https://about.canva.com/canva-for-nonprofits/) program.

Canva for Nonprofits gives you and your team access to all the premium features of Canva Pro, plus team and collaboration tools, for free. This includes the following:

*   Access to premium images, videos, music, and other graphics
*   Downloading designs with [transparent backgrounds](https://www.canva.com/help/transparent-background)
*   Upgraded [Brand Kit](https://www.canva.com/help/brand-kit): create more [color palettes](https://www.canva.com/help/color-palettes), and upload your own [fonts](https://www.canva.com/help/upload-fonts) and [logos](https://www.canva.com/help/brand-logos)
*   Publishing designs as [brand templates](https://www.canva.com/help/publish-team-template)
*   Sharing [designs](https://www.canva.com/help/team-sharing) and [folders](https://www.canva.com/help/share-folders) with your team
*   [Using template](https://www.canva.com/help/share-via-link-or-email) share links 
*   Access to Affinity and use of professional creative photo editing, layout, and illustration. Learn [how to set up Affinity with your account.](https://www.affinity.studio/help/setup-affinity-canva-account/)

Before applying, make sure your Canva account has a valid email address. If your Canva account doesn’t have an email associated to it or you’d like to use a different email from the one you’re currently using, learn more about [Changing your email address](https://www.canva.com/help/change-email).

1.   Check the [eligibility guidelines](https://www.canva.com/canva-for-nonprofits/eligibility-guidelines/) to see if your organization is an eligible nonprofit in your country.
2.   Have an existing Canva account. [Sign up here](https://www.canva.com/) for free if you don’t have one.
3.   Read and review the program’s [Terms and Conditions](https://www.canva.com/canva-for-nonprofits/).
4.   Fill out and submit the [application form](https://www.canva.com/nfp-signup).
5.   Complete the verification process.

If you selected **Nonprofit** from the **signup onboarding** or from the **Your profile**tab on **Settings**, you can also sign up by clicking on **Upgrade to Canva Nonprofits** from the **Billing** tab on **Settings**.

We'll review your application and update you via your provided email address **in 7 to 10 days**.

Note that free access to all premium features of Canva Pro - including team and collaboration tools - is available for one team of up to 50 members. Additional seats are offered at 50% off on [Canva Enterprise](https://www.affinity.studio/help/about-canva-enterprise/).

If you encountered a “Your organization is already using Canva” error, it means that your organization already has an account in Canva.

Reach out to your organization administrator to grant you access to the account.
