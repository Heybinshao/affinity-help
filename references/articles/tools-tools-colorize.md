1.   In the **Canva AI Studio**, on the **Layers**panel, select the image layer you want to edit.
2.   Select the **Colorize Tool**.
3.   Adjust **Saturation**to control the intensity of the generated color.
4.   Adjust **Temperature**to make the color result warmer or cooler.
5.   (Optional) Use the preview controls to compare the result against the original image.
6.   Click **Apply**.

The Colorize Tool enables you to colorize old monochromatic or faded images, typically captured with film cameras in the pre-digital era—although it's not limited to these scenarios. The initial results generated by the Tool are adjustable and often provide an excellent starting point for more advanced photo restoration workflows.

This Tool is applied as a destructive Tool.

For a non-destructive workflow, apply the tool to a duplicate layer rather than the Background layer.

Sometimes when launching the Tool, Affinity's Assistant may inform you that it cannot be applied due to existing color in the image. This is common, as old photographs naturally develop subtle color casts when fading or reacting to environmental factors. In these cases, simply convert your photograph to black and white before applying the Tool.

Another useful editing tip is to increase contrast before applying the Tool, as old photographs naturally fade over time. You may find that applying Colorize to images with enhanced contrast produces more natural-looking results. Contrast can be increased using Brightness/Contrast, Levels, or Curves adjustments, as required.

*   **Saturation**—increases or decreases color saturation.
*   **Temperature**—increases or decreases the color temperature.
