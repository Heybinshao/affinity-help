# Arrowheads - Affinity Help Center

> 官方来源：https://www.affinity.studio/help/curves-shapes-arrowheads/

2.   [Graphic design](https://www.affinity.studio/help/graphic-design/)
3.   Arrowheads

You can add arrowheads to your lines while working with the Pen Tool and shapes.

![Image 1: Arrowheads](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2dvopUItxKtLFObc3pGFGv/c8fc0b9c7afd139db1cc36fae074bbb4/arrowheads.png)

Arrowheads added to strokes.

Strokes and lines can be modified in a number of ways with both arrowheads and optionally tails. Some examples of this include:

*   Change the style of the arrowhead and/or tail.
*   Scale the arrowhead and tail in relation to the stroke, either simultaneously or independently.
*   Control the arrowhead/tail position in relation to the line end.

In the Vector Studio

![Image 2](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2Mejuekyv1U5fQVTYLqGud/edc8c0247142081ce0079de3013e815d/persona_vector.svg)

, you can also add arrowheads to pencil strokes drawn with the Pencil Tool.

1.   With a line or curve selected, do one of the following: 
    *   In the Vector Studio![Image 3](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/2Mejuekyv1U5fQVTYLqGud/edc8c0247142081ce0079de3013e815d/persona_vector.svg)  or Layout Studio![Image 4](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4BbduuJ3zEK1FT2Ohg1aLs/98f305a0ad579187aa758ae37dc956e6/layout_studio.svg) , navigate to the **Stroke** panel.
    *   In the Pixel Studio![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/x0qUKV0QmljBpXjlKaAyD/48b1503c7d6cc8f05d91c6af70f31b85/persona_photo.svg) , click **Edit stroke settings**![Image 6: Edit stroke settings](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/LAIAniI5tfK3bknzYOUQB/9af81e28e315cea94436ab657c986f85/stroke.svg) on the context toolbar.

2.   Select an arrowhead style from the **Start** and/or **End** pop-up menus.
3.   Choose where to position the start and end styles from **Place arrow within the line**![Image 7](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4ovru5tVWJ5iuDrO7UvFB4/7f2aea7b265b7931b3ac620f16e4996c/arrowhead_inside.svg)  and **Place arrow at the end of the line**![Image 8](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/4Iz9W9HjMmLN3ViR6YfPBW/5433be7a44624a87e7e0b871e4b16b39/arrowhead_outside.svg) .
4.   With arrowhead styles selected, you can then enter a percentage to the **Start** and **End** styles to adjust the size of your selected arrowheads in proportion with the stroke width.

To swap the Start and End points click **Swap arrowhead with tail**

![Image 9](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3M2d5eMwr98NZJE9CDP30X/b74977a4478981774d07bf298700e003/arrowhead_swap.svg)

.

Click **Clear arrowheads**

![Image 10](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3m8ciDtLqf07Yrma1i0j4x/202c1d508104bbb0a1290c5be7725eae/trash_can.svg)

 to clear arrowhead settings if needed.
