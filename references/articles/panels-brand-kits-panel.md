# Canva Brand Kits panel

The Brand Kits panel hosts all your Brand Kit from Canva. You can swap between Brand Kits, use the elements in your current Brand Kit in your Affinity design, and upload pro image assets designed in Affinity to your current Brand Kit on Canva.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/panels-brand-kits-panel/#where-to-find-it)

### Where to find it

For Mac/Windows: On the **Window** menu, select **Canva > Canva Brand Kits**.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/panels-brand-kits-panel/#about-brand-kit-in-affinity)

## About Brand Kit in Affinity

Being able to access your Brand Kit on Canva lets you keep your designs on-brand. If you're a Brand Designer, you'll be able to develop the brand by uploading designs to the main Brand Kit.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/panels-brand-kits-panel/#options)

## Options

Desktop app

The following options are available on the panel:

*   **Search**—filters the Brand Kit list to those that match your criteria. Type directly in the text box.
*   **Brand Kit list**—shows the Brand Kits available to you on Canva. Select your chosen Brand Kit to work with from the pop-up menu.
*     ![Image 5](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/5iOChGTdKjQVqHOcdsqZA0/6fefc7a0413254f40e53bc188bbe994c/viewBrandKitOnCanva.svg)       **Edit Brand Kit in Canva**—click to display the active Brand Kit on Canva in the panel.

![Image 6](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/6UxpXBt5miovw347YaOPUz/6f8e3e8d9b379daa0bdc5a3ac6ff23e8/panel_preferences.svg)

 The following options are available on the **Panel Preferences** menu:
*   **Refresh**—click to manually update the local Brand Kit content if it isn't synchronized between Canva and Affinity.
*   **Panels**—opens a dialog where you can quickly set the visibility of all panels in the current Studio.
*   **Close**—hides the current panel.
*   **Close Panel Group**—hides the current panel and any others grouped with it.
