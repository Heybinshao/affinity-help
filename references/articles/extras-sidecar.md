# Using Sidecar (Mac)

Sidecar allows you to extend or mirror your Mac desktop onto your iPad’s display. This feature lets you draw on your iPad using an Apple Pencil to edit documents in apps like Affinity on your Mac.

This feature is only available in Affinity for Mac.

![Image 5: Mirrored display](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/BeyPVR56PV9qH21eE0Qut/b4293521ccd2b06c965fc802b96e05c3/sidecar2.jpg)

A mirrored display.

![Image 6: Extended display](https://images.ctfassets.net/3p2fxa94bzao/3irJX0IbdlkTkhJW6tLO93/4cff846734b96423587ac3c06c84f465/sidecar3.jpg)

An extended display.

Sidecar works with one iPad at a time and can be used alongside other external displays, depending on how many displays your Mac supports. However, if your Mac supports only one external display, you can't use Sidecar and another external display simultaneously.

Sidecar also displays modifier keys (**⌘**, **⌥**, **⌃(ctrl)** and **⇧**) and other supplementary controls on your iPad's screen. This lets you perform common operations without reaching for your Mac's keyboard.

For full instructions on using Sidecar, see Use an iPad as a second display for your Mac (https://support.apple.com/HT210380) at Apple Support.

[](https://www.affinity.studio/help/extras-sidecar/#using-sidecar-with-affinity)

## Using Sidecar with Affinity

When using Sidecar, Affinity for Mac supports pressure-sensitive input from Apple Pencil on your iPad's screen.

Affinity's panels can be dragged between your Mac and iPad displays as needed.

Affinity's Tools panel can be relocated by deselecting **View > Tools > Dock Tools**.

With Sidecar's Touch Bar feature turned on, context-sensitive controls for Affinity are displayed at the top or bottom of your iPad's display.
